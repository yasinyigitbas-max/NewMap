# Mixamo clips for player_v4, by exact Mixamo name

Made 25 Nov 2026 for downloading by hand. This replaces the clip lists in `Mixamo rig and re-download.md`, which used the Drive file names; names with a number in brackets are not Mixamo names, which is why the scripted download picked wrong clips.

How to use it: type the **Search** text into Mixamo, pick the clip whose description matches **Pick**, and check its length. Save it under the **Save as** name. Settings for every clip: FBX Binary, Without Skin, 30 frames per second, keyframe reduction none, "In Place" off.

The **Sure?** column: `yes` means the name is unique or the clip was matched by measurement; `by length` means several clips share the name and this one was picked because its length matches; `check length` or `unsure` means look at the length before downloading. Names and descriptions come from a public copy of the Mixamo catalogue and may differ slightly on the live site.
