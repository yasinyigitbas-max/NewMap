## Models in the game (previous set)
Every car is one merged mesh using ONE shared material: `vehicles/car_paint.gdshader` on the 1024 atlas `vehicles/models/Textures/car_atlas.png`, built by `tools/red_gen_car_atlas.py`. The atlas alpha is an area mask (1 = paint, 0.5 = glass with low roughness and a fake sky reflection, 0 = other), so only the body paint takes the per-car random paint colour; glass (dark, slightly blue), lights, trim and tyres keep their own colours.
The CC0 GLBs were built with Blender 4.5 by `tools/red_build_car_models_blender.py` (sources in `/workspace/sports_dl`; renames parts generically, centres each car with the front at +Z, bakes flat material colours into swatch cells, decimates to mobile budget).
Fiamma R and Fiamma GT are **original smooth models** built by `tools/red_build_fiamma_blender.py` (Blender bpy module + numpy): a dense control cage of cross-sections is turned into its cubic B-spline / Catmull-Clark limit surface and sampled on a regular grid, so paint/glass/light borders stay crisp; wheels have big 10-spoke rims. Van and City Bus are **original models** generated by `tools/red_gen_vehicles_original.py` (pure Python + numpy, writes GLB directly). Every vertex comes from the script's own parameter tables: lofted cross-sections along the length, wheel-arch cut-outs, surface decals for glass/lights/grilles/seams, and simple boxes/cylinders. No third-party mesh data was read or used. Parts: `body`, `wheel_fl/fr/rl/rr`; one material; UVs point into a 16-cell swatch (paint, paint2, glass, head/tail/amber lights, trim, tyres, rims).

## De-identification (what was changed per car)
- **Fiamma R / Fiamma GT, Van, City Bus: original designs (2026-10-22).** Built from scratch by `tools/red_gen_vehicles_original.py`; the old CC-BY meshes and the de-identified Fiamma texture were deleted.
  - Fiamma R: mid-engine wedge hypercar, cab-forward teardrop canopy, slanted blade headlamps, deep side intakes ahead of the rear wheels, louvred engine cover, two-tier slim tail bars with a short vertical drop, carbon diffuser with fins, twin centre exhausts, low strut wing. Not modelled on any single real car.
  - Fiamma GT: front-engine fastback grand tourer, long bonnet with vent strip, cabin set back, sculpted side scallop, three-slat gill behind the front wheel, long horizontal lamps plus a separate low light strip, wide low mouth with one bar, full-width tail bar, quad exhausts, lip spoiler. Different body from R (not a longer tail).
  - Van: generic high-roof delivery van (short bonnet, raked screen, sliding door, barn rear doors, tall rear lamps). City Bus: generic low-floor city bus (one-piece screen, blank destination band, dark pillars, two glazed kerb-side doors, roof A/C pod).
  - No badges, logos, names or plate text (plates are blank).
  - Before/after render: `/workspace/red_cars_shots/remake_before_after.png`; line-up: `/workspace/red_cars_shots/lineup_v2.png`
- **Everyday cars, taxi, Midibus:** original generic designs (Quaternius / Innerscene, CC0). No badges, plates blank. Nothing else needed changing.

## First-person ride interiors (red-dc-vehint)
- taxi / volt_s (flatmate car) cabins: the original cabin geometry of the models above (Daniel Zhabotinsky, CC BY 4.0), i.e. the triangles listed in `models/interior_tris.json`, baked to `models/lod/<id>_int.res` by `tools/cars/red_bake_vehicle_interiors.gd`. These are only shown while the player rides.
- bus_city cabin: the MaG80 city bus cabin (CC BY 4.0) plus a generated inner lining, poles and handrails.
- bus_mini cabin: hand-built in `vehicle_interior.gd`; the Innerscene minibus (CC0) has no cabin.
- Seated taxi driver: the game's own NPC hero model with IK hands on the wheel.
- No suitable CC0 car interior was found that fits these bodies, so the existing CC-BY cabins are reused. They fit the bodies and windows exactly.
