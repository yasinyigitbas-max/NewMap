# Rooms on all shops: pictures (18 Nov, cloud)
Yasin: "i want them on all shops" (real windows, see-through doors, the shop's sign). Every shop of the map now has a
room (`interiors/assignment_all.json`, rooms in `interiors/rooms/`: 12 hand-made, 150 generated by
`tools/city/rooms/room_gen.gd`). Pictures by `tools/city/rooms/gen_shots.gd` on the raw pieces, the doors and windows
opened in memory (the bake will cut them; the locked pieces are never written).
- `pilot/amber_*_closed` = the high street as it was (painted windows), `amber_*` = with the rooms, `amber_*_night`
  = the rooms lit at night (sun down; no street lamps in this preview).
- `pilot/<shop>_street`, `<shop>_in`: each shop from the pavement and inside, from its door. `centre/`: the city centre.
- `merged_vs_built_bakery.jpg`: a room as built (top) and merged for the phone (bottom, `tools/city/rooms/room_merge.gd`).
Cloud flaws: no shadows (software GPU), weathered buildings drawn about 40 % too dark, sky reflections off.
NOT in the streamed city / phone yet: the bake has to cut the openings and pack the rooms (docs/look_patches/rooms_bake.md).
