Yasin: "i want them on all shops" (real rooms, real windows, see-through doors, the shop's sign). The rooms exist now
(`map_dc/coronado/city_kit/interiors/rooms/`: 12 hand-made, 150 generated by `tools/city/rooms/room_gen.gd`, indoor

## What the bake has to do (in `bake_runtime.gd`)
1. **Know which building has which room.** `interiors/assignment_all.json` lists every shop: `piece`, `label` (the
   building node's name in the piece), `door_index`, and the room scene: `built` (hand-made) or `g_<id>` (generated).
   Several rooms can sit in one building (rows); each room scene's meta `cut` lists the openings it fills as
   `[facade, along]`.
2. **Cut those openings out of the building's near mesh** at bake time, in `_building()` before the near mesh goes to
   `lib/` (it is hashed by content, so a cut building is a new lib mesh; uncut copies of the model keep sharing theirs).
   The cut is the same as `interiors/rooms/room_shots.gd` `_open_ground_floor` and `tools/city/rooms/gen_shots.gd`
   `_open`: drop the triangles whose UV centre lies in the opening's atlas region (`city_layout.json` O_*) and whose
   position lies in the opening's rectangle (`room_contracts.json` openings: along, w, y0, y1, plane). Locked piece
   files are never written: the cut lives in the baked data only (lock test keeps passing).
   - Mid tier (60-220 m): leave it uncut (the room is drawn to 62 m only; the mid tier paints the windows shut).
3. **Put the merged room into the cell** under the building node (transform = the building's), from
   `runtime/rooms/<room>.scn` made by `tools/city/rooms/room_merge.gd` (prototype here; best moved into the bake as a
   step after the cells). Every room geometry already has `visibility_range_end = 62` (the near tier's end).
4. **Lights.** A room carries 2-6 real omni lights (fixtures + daylight at the window). With many rooms near the camera
   that is too many for the light grid (32). Proposal: do not bake the room's OmniLight3D into the cell; record them as
   light anchors (like the lamps: `cell.lights`, kind 2 = room) and let `city_lights.gd` give real lights to the
   nearest rooms only (e.g. 2 rooms x 3 lights inside the 20-light budget); the other rooms show their emissive panels
   and glass only. Not built, not measured.
5. **Collision:** none needed yet (Yasin: rooms are visuals first). Later: the room's walls as boxes.
6. **Warm-up:** add one merged room (shell + props materials) to `lib/warm.scn`.
