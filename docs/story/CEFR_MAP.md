# CEFR map: what each mission is built to show

The game follows the CEFR levels and the usual general-English syllabus order (the sequence most A1–B2 coursebooks
share). The player never sees this table. It is for:
- the writers: what a scene's lines may use;
- the Pocket lessons: which lesson the game offers after a mission;
- the translation tiers: which structure the syntax tier explains in that mission.

How to read it:
- **New**: the structure the mission is built around. It appears again and again in the scene, and the player must
  *say* it at least once.
- **Recycled**: older structures the scene leans on, so nothing learned is ever dropped.
- **Ceiling**: NPCs may speak above it (fog plus translation tiers help). The player's own lines never go above the
  New column.
- **Pocket after**: the lesson offered when the mission ends. It is never forced.
