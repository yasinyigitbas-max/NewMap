# FIRST GEAR — Script 4: Act 4, *The Last Drive*, and the Epilogue
*Friday 3 July – Friday 31 July. A1 review, under pressure.*
Format as in Script 1. Act 4 contract for `YOU (say)`: at most 10 words a sentence, everything in the A1 syllabus, and
nothing above it. Lou now speaks in short correct sentences, slowly; each one costs him, and the actor lets us hear it.

- **Where:** the Fourth of July on the island: the parade on Amber Avenue; Hank's Diner; Rosa's Taqueria and Ocean Park
  Skate; the bus stop (Marisol); Bay Medical Plaza Urgent Care; Hank's again at four.

- **Play:** RECRUIT (an open-world run of short asks, each person remembering Lou in one past sentence); the parade
