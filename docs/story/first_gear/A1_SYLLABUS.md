# FIRST GEAR: the A1 syllabus, mission by mission
The checklist follows the CEFR A1 level as the common syllabuses set it out (the Council of Europe descriptors and the
companion volume's mediation scales; the British Council / EAQUALS Core Inventory for A1; the Cambridge A1 topic
lists). Every item has the mission that **teaches** it (the player meets it many times there and says it at least three
times) and the missions where it **comes back**.

## 1. The rules for every line the player says (the A1 contract)
Writers check every `YOU (say)` line against these. NPCs speak naturally; the fog, the tiers and `(slow)` versions
carry the player.

1. **Structures:** only those taught in this mission or before (section 3). No exceptions.
2. **Length:** at most 7 words a sentence in the Prologue and Act 1, 8 in Act 2, 10 in Acts 3–4. Two sentences in a row
   are fine; three only at the peaks (M15, M18, M20, E2).
3. **Words:** the A1 word list (about 700 words: the Oxford 3000 A1 band / Cambridge Movers), plus proper names, plus
   **the driving words** (car, drive, driver, keys, left, right, stop, slow, fast, turn, park, bus, licence, seat belt),
   plus at most five new words a mission, each shown first with a picture or an object.
4. **Fixed chunks** (taught as wholes before their grammar): *Hello. / Goodbye. / Please. / Thank you. / Sorry? /
   Slower, please. / I don't understand. / Excuse me. / Here you are. / Can I have …, please? / No problem. / Let's go. /
   Keep the change. / Nice to meet you. / See you tomorrow. / I'm sorry.* (sympathy) */ Happy birthday.*
5. **Joiners:** *and* from Act 1; *but* and *or* from Act 2; *because* from Act 3. Nothing else.
6. **Tenses:** present *be*, *have got*, present simple, *can*, imperatives (Prologue–Act 2); present continuous (M12);
   *was / were* (M14); a small past simple (M15: *went, saw, took, had, came, drove, hit, sang, hid, gave, said, was /
   were* and regular *-ed*). **No future tenses:** the future is *Let's …*, *I want to …*, or a time word with the
   present (*"Tomorrow I drive Lou."*).
7. **Never fail on grammar.** A wrong but clear line works; the NPC answers the meaning and says the right form back
   once, naturally (a recast).
8. **Lou's lines are inside the contract too**, one step below the player (single words in Act 1, short broken phrases
   in Acts 2–3, short correct sentences in Act 4). He is the character the player always understands.

## 2. Mission by mission
**Teaches** is what the mission is built round. **Topic words** are its vocabulary. **Skills** names the reading,
writing, listening, speaking and mediation the mission asks for. **Five Cards** is the practice test offered at the end.
