# ALIEN STATUS: missions, part 4 (Chapter 4: Make It, days 31 to 40, and the epilogue)

Version 2 (GTA rework). Read `MISSIONS_1_PROLOGUE_CH1.md` first for how a mission is written. **CUT** = a
cutscene (clips in `CUTSCENES.md`). **PLAY** = you have the controls. `(old)` = the old game's line, word for
word.

The last chapter runs in a straight line. Every mission gives back something from the first hour: the docks, the
camp's orders, the house tour, the hospital drive, the doctor's *should*, and the question in the small room.

## 4.1 FOUR HUNDRED HOURS
**From:** Orla's old line *"Our day is four hundred hours. We are always tired."* **Where:** the ship; Glorietta
Bay off the marina's fuel dock; the east ring up to 10th St; home. **Giver:** the green dot. **Length:** 25 min.

**You do:** choose where a spaceship lands, talk it down, and get two aliens home across a town the Navy is
already searching.

- **CUT 4.1-1 "It Must Sleep"** (30 s). **03:00.** The green light flickers. The hum is wrong. On the ship the
  floor leans and something is ringing. **Orla**, calm the way people are calm in hospitals: *"The ship is
  tired. Four hundred hours. It must sleep. On the ground. Where? You know the ground."*
- **PLAY: the glass floor again.** In 1.6 you named these places. Now you choose between them, and say why:
  **YOU:** *"Not there. There are people."* (the ocean beach) *"Not there. There is a police boat."* (the Ferry
  Landing) *"Not there. There are lights."* (the docks) *"There. It is quiet."* (the bay by the marina). A wrong
  choice is allowed. It makes the way home longer and louder.
  Then Orla flies, badly, and you call it: **YOU:** *"Left. Left! Down. Slow! Stop. Down."*
- **CUT 4.1-2 "Splash"** (25 s). The ship goes into Glorietta Bay at four in the morning with no splash at all,
  and settles. A green glow under the water, fading. The marina boats rock at their ropes. You swim. Zib floats
  on its back like a cork. Orla sinks, walks along the bottom, and comes up the boat ramp with weed on her head.
  On the fuel dock's lamp, a gull, and far across the bay, a searchlight coming on.
- **PLAY: home before sunrise.** Seven hundred metres uphill: Glorietta Blvd, the ring park, the back streets to
  10th. Zib in the jacket. Orla in a blue boat cover. Navy vans are quartering the east side with lights off.
  - **Bird view** from the wires shows which street has a van in it.
  - Quietly: behind parked cars, through gardens, over a wall (two aliens who have to be told *"Up." "Down."
    "Wait."*), **"Look!"** to turn a patrol.
  - Loudly: take a van. Four stars, two passengers who think it is wonderful.
  - A paper boy on a bike, who sees everything: *"Cool costume."* **Orla:** *"Thank you. It is my skin."*
    **YOU:** *"Orla. Quiet."*
- **CUT 4.1-3 "New Flatmates"** (60 s). The hall. {H}, in a dressing gown, with a glass of water, at the foot of
  the stairs. Two aliens in the doorway. A very long time. **YOU:** *"{H}. This is Orla. This is Zib."* **Orla**,
  who has heard this scene in a Report and practised it: *"Hi! You must be my new flatmate. Nice to meet you!"*
  `(H's own old lines)` **{H}**, from far away: *"...Welcome home. Make yourself... at home."* `(old words,
  slower)` Then: *"The jelly. The jelly was REAL."* From the kitchen sink: *"Blub."* Upstairs, the forms come
  off the wall. **{H}**, at the attic door, to you: *"That door is their room. It's locked. Please don't go in,
  OK?"* `(old, turned round)`

**Carries:** *there is / there are* with a reason; orders under pressure; introducing people.
**Gold:** the bay first time; home before the sun; no stars.

## W4. THE PRESIDENT
**From:** new. **Where:** the Crest Lookout; home. **Giver:** W. **Length:** 12 min.

**You do:** bring the Coronado Bird Club its second and third members.

- **CUT W4-1 "Thirty Years"** (40 s). The van. Finch has taken every photograph off the wall. One left: you, at
  the gate. **Finch:** *"I was right. I was right. And I can't tell anybody. Who do I tell? Who?"* **YOU:**
  *"Come to my house."*
- **PLAY: the drive.** Finch in the passenger seat, counting birds out of the window, twice each. Tonight, with
  the Navy out, he is useful: thirty years of radar. *"Left. They watch the right. Now slow. Now go."*
- **CUT W4-2 "Members"** (60 s). The attic. Finch in the doorway. Zib looks up from the notebook and waves, six
  fingers. A bird on the roof window waves too, the same wave. Finch sits down on the floor, slowly. **Orla:**
  *"You are the man who counts. We know you. You are very good. You found four hundred and twelve."* **Finch:**
  *"...How many are there?"* **Orla:** *"Four hundred and thirteen."* He laughs until he has to take his hat
  off. Zib gives him a card it has made: **CORONADO BIRD CLUB. MEMBER.**

**Opens:** Finch on the radio in 4.3; the Bird Club's last rank.

## 4.2 CLASS TRIP
**From:** Grace's class (3.4); Goo. **Where:** home; Christ Church Community Center; Coronado University: the
Quad, the Science Building, the campus garage; 6th St. **Giver:** G. **Length:** 35 min.

**You do:** lose your translator and your kit, plan a break-in with a class of beginners, get into a lab as a
cleaner, and shoot your way out with a jelly the size of a dog.

- **CUT 4.2-1 "The Drains"** (35 s). Morning. A plumbers' van outside. Two men in clean overalls and good shoes.
  *"Water company. There's a problem with the drains."* Ten minutes later they carry the pipe from under the
  kitchen sink out to the van in a sealed box. Something in the box says *"Class is not over!"* The van has no
  name on it. The drop in your ear goes cold and still.
- **PLAY: silence.** No small voice under the words. No kit: point the Stopper and nothing stops. The island is
  in English only, and you walk down Amber Avenue, and somebody asks you the time, and a bus calls a stop, and
  you understand them. Not all of it. Enough. (The game says nothing about this. The officer's slow phone app
  is still there for anyone who needs it.)
- **CUT 4.2-2 "The Plan"** (70 s). In the attic Zib turns the dial of the old tape recorder until it hums, points
  it west, and draws: a tall building, a square, a flag. **YOU:** *"The university."* Tuesday, the hall, the
  board. Grace gives you the chalk. Eleven people with a few hundred words between them, so the plan is made of
  small words, and everybody puts in the one thing they have:
  **Mei:** *"Cleaners go everywhere. Nobody looks."* **Lina:** two grey coats from Shift Linen & Uniforms.
  **Omar:** the canteen's trolley. *"Guards are hungry. Always."* **Yusuf:** *"I sit. I see. I call."*
  **{F}:** the library van, parked in the campus garage. **Sal:** *"I drive. Fast. Finally!"* **Ivan:**
  *"Door."* **Grace** writes it up as a lesson: *First. Then. After that. At ten o'clock.*
  (If you said *yes* to Rhodes, this scene starts differently. **Ivan:** *"We are friends?"* It is a question
  now. **YOU:** *"I was wrong. I am sorry."* A long look. Then he pulls out two chairs.)
- **PLAY: in.** Night, the Quad, the Science Building. You and Mei, grey coats, a mop bucket. No kit. You get
  through by listening:
  - two guards at the lift: *"Coffee at ten. Five minutes."*: your window;
  - a scientist on a phone: *"...third floor, the cold room, at the end."*: your door;
  - a guard: *"Hey. You're new."* **YOU:** *"Yes. I am new here."* `(old, from the taxi)` He holds the door for
    the bucket;
  - Yusuf, on the phone, very slowly: *"One. Man. Goes. Left."*;
  - Omar's trolley reaches the guard room at exactly ten.
- **CUT 4.2-3 "Class Is Not Over"** (40 s). The cold room. A glass tank under a lamp. A Navy language expert sits
  in front of it holding up cards: a cat, a house, a ball. Goo is not answering. Goo is teaching: **Goo:** *"No!
  Wrong! 'It is a ball.' Say it again. Class is not over!"* He has been in there six hours. He looks grateful to
  see a cleaner. Goo has spent a day eating scientists' words. Goo fills the tank. The glass cracks. Cold in
  your ear: *"Student! You came! You are late!"* And out of the tank, with a wet sound, your kit, and something
  new.
- **PLAY: out.** Alarms. The building fills with men in white, and tonight they have stun rifles. Floor by
  floor down the stairs: the Puddler, the Hummer through glass lab doors, and the new one, **Goo bombs**: a
  green patch on the floor that holds every boot that steps in it (**"Stay!"** for a whole corridor). At a
  locked fire door: **"Puddle."** on yourself, and you and Goo go under it between two pairs of boots.
  The campus garage. Ivan is at the door, as promised, holding it shut from the outside with his back.
- **PLAY: the run.** Sal's taxi, 6th St east, three vans and a helicopter. The class is already on the route:
  a food trolley rolling across Union Plaza; a rope of bed sheets across a windscreen at C Avenue; a book bike
  in the middle of a junction. Goo bombs out of the back window. The car wash.
- **CUT 4.2-4 "Tomorrow: School"** (20 s). Home. Goo goes back down the sink and is quiet for almost a minute.
  Then: *"Tomorrow: school."*

**Carries:** *first, then, after that*; times; a floor, a room, a time, heard with no help.
**Gold:** in inside the five minutes; never stopped twice; the vans lost before Amber Avenue.
**Opens:** **Goo bombs**; **"Puddle."**

## 4.3 LIGHTS OUT
**From:** the docks (P2); the camp's morning sport (P3); the hospital drive (2.4). **Where:** home to the marina
by the east ring; the fuel dock; Glorietta Bay; under the bridge; round the whole island. **Day 38.**
**Length:** 30 min.

**You do:** talk through a checkpoint, hold a dock with everyone you know, and fly a spaceship that only listens
to you.

- **CUT 4.3-1 "Tonight"** (30 s). Evening. From the attic window: floodlights on Glorietta Bay, the harbour
  patrol boat off the fuel dock, helicopters in a slow circle, the bridge shut. **Finch**, on a radio he has
  built into a biscuit tin: *"He's found it. He's found it. Tonight or never."* {H} comes up the stairs with car
  keys in a hand that works again. **{H}:** *"I drive. You talk."*
- **PLAY: the drive.** {H} at the wheel, two shapes under a blanket in the back, you in the passenger seat. It is
  2.4 the other way round: **YOU:** *"Left here. Slow down. Stop: the light is red. Now go. The second street on
  the right."*
  **The checkpoint**, Glorietta Blvd. One patrol car, one officer, a torch. *"Evening. Road's closed. Where are
  you going?"* **YOU:** *"Good evening, officer. We are going to the marina. My friend has a boat."* *"Who's in
  the back?"* **YOU:** *"My friends. They are tourists. They are very tired."* **Orla**, from under the blanket:
  *"We are always tired."* `(old)` *"ID?"* A licence and a release paper with two stamps. He reads the number
  of arrests on it. If it is small he lifts the tape. If it is large he whistles, and you drive through the
  tape.
- **CUT 4.3-2 "It Knows Your Voice"** (45 s). The fuel dock. The water in front of it bulges green and the ship
  comes up, dripping, dark, slow: it needs four minutes to wake. Rhodes on a loudspeaker from the patrol boat,
  as fast as ever. Headlights behind you on the marina road: a taxi, a tow truck, a library van, a bicycle.
  Sal. Ivan with a tyre iron. {H} with a frying pan from Little Frenchy. Omar, Lina, Mei, Yusuf with his board
  under his arm. Grace. {F}. On the dock's roof, in a fishing hat: Finch. **Ivan:** *"Door."*
- **PLAY: four minutes.** The last fight, and the biggest. Men in white come down the marina road, along the
  floats, and off two boats. Everything you own is live and everyone you know is in it:
  - you: the whole kit, cover behind fuel pumps, boat hulls and bollards;
  - Ivan on the gangway, where they can only come one at a time;
  - Sal's taxi, driven at people;
  - Finch calling it from the roof, twice each: *"Left side! Left side! Two on the boats! Two on the boats!"*;
  - bird view from every lamp;
  - Zib, at the hatch, handing out Goo bombs like biscuits.
  Nobody on your side goes down for long. Orla sees to that.
- **CUT 4.3-3 "Up"** (30 s). The board inside the ship is full of green and none of it is right. **Orla:** *"The
  ship listens to English. Tonight the air is full of English. Navy English. It hears them. It does not hear
  us."* She looks at you. *"Your voice it knows. You talk to it every night."* You sit down in a chair that was
  not made for you.
- **PLAY: the flight.** Your voice and your thumbs. Every order is one the camp guard gave you on a cold
  morning, and one you were shouted at on a dock:
  - **YOU:** *"Up!"* Off the water. *"Lights off!"* *"Forward! Fast!"* The patrol boat turns too late.
  - The bridge, shut, lit, full of blue lights. **YOU:** *"Down! Under!"* Between its legs, a metre off the
    water.
  - Helicopters. **YOU:** *"Left! Right! Up! Stop!"* They go past. *"Go!"* The ship's own Stopper works on
    rotor blades for exactly two seconds.
  - Then the whole island, once, low and fast, because the only clear way out is the long way round: the east
    beaches, the hospital and its helipad, the Ferry Landing, the steel works and its water tower, the
    container depot where you came over the side of a ship (twenty people are coming down a rope net tonight
    too), the base fence, a yard, tents, a board, very small. The stadium. (**Zib** points. **YOU:** *"Yes. The
    hat."*) The hotel's red roof, the old lookout tower, and up the hill over 10th Street, where a kitchen
    light is on.
- **CUT 4.3-4 "You Speak Too Fast"** (50 s). The radio. **Rhodes:** *"Vessel, you are ordered to land, bring it
  down nice and easy and nobody gets hurt."* The ship does nothing. **Orla**, slowly, kindly: *"It cannot hear
  you. You speak too fast."* A long silence. Then, for the second time in his life: **Rhodes:** *"Please. Come.
  Down."* The ship slows. It heard that. It begins to turn. **YOU:** *"Up."* It knows your voice. Cloud. Then no
  cloud. The island is a small bright shape on a black sea with a bridge like a thread. Zib's clock: **DAY 39.**

**Carries:** the first ten words you learned, as fast as you can say them; directions; staying calm with an
officer.
**Gold:** nobody on your side puddled or netted at the dock; under the bridge without a touch; never in a
searchlight.

## 4.4 WHAT SHOULD WE DO NOW?
**From:** ch8, *"What should I do now?"* **Where:** the ship; your roof. **Giver:** Orla. **Length:** 8 min.

**You do:** explain one word, and give the advice that ends the story.

- **CUT 4.4-1 "A Word on a Paper"** (60 s, with your lines in it). Quiet. Orla has your file on the wall again.
  **Orla:** *"Tomorrow they ask: who are you. And it says here: ALIEN. Now you have many words. Tell us."* In 1.6
  you had no way to say it. Now: **YOU:** *"Alien is a word on a paper."* *"It means: a person from another
  place."* *"I am from another place. You are from another place."* **Orla:** *"So we are the same."* **YOU:**
  *"Yes."* **Orla:** *"Then why do they have a different word for you?"* **YOU:** *"Next question, please."*
  `(old)` Zib laughs. It is the first sound Zib has made.
- **PLAY: the choice.** **Orla:** *"We cannot land again. He knows the bay."* And, the way {H} asked it on a
  sofa with a bandaged hand: *"What should we do now?"* `(old, with "we")`
  - **YOU:** *"You should go home."* **Orla:** *"You're right. We should go."* Goo is asked, and chooses the
    sink.
  - **YOU:** *"You should stay."* The attic, the locked door, the jacket and the hat. **Orla:** *"OK. We should
    be quiet."* She will not be.
  - **YOU:** *"You should ask."* The slow way. *"I tried legal ways, but I was rejected."* `(old)` **YOU:** *"It
    is slow. You should ask anyway."* **Orla:** *"Zib will write the form. Zib writes very well."*
- **CUT 4.4-2 "Good Human"** (30 s). First light. They set you down on your own roof by the attic window.
  **Orla:** *"Good human."* `(old)` **YOU:** *"Not 'human'. {NAME}."* **Orla:** *"Good {NAME}. Sleep well!"*
  `(old words)` The ship goes up without a sound. On the chimney, a pigeon watches it go, then looks at you.

## 4.5 WHO ARE YOU?
**From:** the interrogation room (P4); the gate officer's *"I really hope you make it."* **Where:** Island Legal
Aid; Coronado County Courthouse. **Giver:** O. **Length:** 15 min.

**You do:** answer the first question of the game.

- **CUT 4.5-1 "Slowly, Please"** (90 s). **Day 40.** The lawyer from 3.10, more folders: *"I can stand next to
  you. I can't talk for you."* The courtroom. The judge. Reyes, with a folder. Rhodes, with photographs. The
  benches behind you are as full as your forty days made them. **Rhodes** goes first, standing, quick and
  friendly: bay, lights, unidentified, persons of interest, a matter of national...
  **Judge:** *"Commander. Slowly, please."* `(your line, from the first ten minutes of the game)`
  He tries. Said slowly, in plain words, with everybody looking, it is this: **Rhodes:** *"There was... a green
  jelly. In a kitchen sink. It gave my man a lesson."* Somebody laughs. Then the room. His photographs: a bus.
  A car park. A child in a hat on a beach. **Judge:** *"Thank you, Commander."*
- **CUT 4.5-2 "The Last Report"** (60 s). **Reyes:** *"The host's reports, Your Honour. And one more thing."* He
  reads your number of arrests. If it is large, the judge writes for some time. {H} stands up at the back.
  *"Can I read the last one?"* It is your save file, in {H}'s words: *"Day 10. Likes pizza. Ran after a man for
  it. Day 11. Got a job. Day 13. Lost it. Day 14. Got another one. Drove me to hospital. Had no licence. Got
  one. Day 24. Paid the rent. Day 29. There is no more Vince. Washes the dishes. Now. English: five. This is my
  friend."* Then, one line each, whoever you earned: **Sal:** *"Not a sailor. Better."* **Ms. Bell:** *"On time.
  Every day."* **Grace:** *"A good student. A better teacher."* **Ivan**, standing: *"Friend."* **Finch:** *"He
  can count. He can count."* **Mr. Dunn**, at the back, surprised to be there: *"Late once. Once."*
- **PLAY: the question.** **Judge:** *"Now you. Who are you?"* The last scramble, and the biggest: the screen
  fills with your own forty days in word tiles. Your name, your age, where you live, the job you had and the
  jobs you have, what you can do, what you like, who your friends are, what you did, what you are going to do.
  Build five sentences, or eight, or twelve. Say them. There is no trap tile in this one and nobody is counting.
- **CUT 4.5-3 "You Made It"** (60 s). The game plays your answer back, in your voice, over the small room from
  the first hour: the metal desk, the tired officer, the silence where your answer should have been. The stamp.
  **DAY 40.** *"Resident."* (With a long list of arrests: *"Thirty more days. Then we talk again."* The game
  goes on; the clock comes back, gentler.) The steps outside. **Reyes:** *"You made it."* `(old: "I really hope
  you make it.")` Sal's taxi at the kerb. **Sal:** *"Where to?"* `(old)` **YOU:** *"Home."* **Sal:** *"On Uncle
  Sam?"* **YOU:** *"No. This one is on me."* `(old gold line)` On the courthouse roof, as the taxi pulls out,
  every bird on the island, in rows.

**Carries:** everything. The only test in the game, and the player wrote the questions.
**Gold:** a full bench.

# EPILOGUE: DAY 41

## E1. QUICK QUESTION
**Where:** home. **Length:** 5 min, then for as long as you like.

- **CUT E1-1 "Pizza or Tacos"** (40 s). Morning. No day number in the corner. The kitchen: Goo in the sink, the
  size of a cat, its own certificate on the fridge next to yours. {H} at the cooker, two hands working. **{H}:**
  *"Quick question!"* `(old)` You wait for it. **{H}:** *"So, what do you want? Pizza or tacos?"* `(old)`
- What the choice in 4.4 left behind:
  - **Go home:** every third night, at three, for one second, a green light crosses your ceiling.
  - **Stay:** two more for dinner. Orla wants a job. The field trips go on.
  - **Ask:** a week later Reyes rings. A parcel has come to the Release Desk from very far away: an application,
    four hundred pages, in perfect handwriting. *"I'm going to need a bigger folder."*
- The island is open. Your old job, the one you told Sal about on day ten, no longer says *"Not yet."*
- And the birds are still there. And the buoys. Whatever you chose, the Report still comes every night, and
  nobody has told you who else is watching it. For that, you have to swim (OPEN_WORLD.md, *The edge*).
