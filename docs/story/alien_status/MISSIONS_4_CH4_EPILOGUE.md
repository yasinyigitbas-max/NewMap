# ALIEN STATUS: missions, part 4 (Chapter 4: Make It, days 31 to 40, and the epilogue)

Read `MISSIONS_1_PROLOGUE_CH1.md` first for how a mission is written. `(old)` = the old game's line, word for word.

The last chapter runs in a straight line. Every mission gives back something from the first hour: the camp's
orders, the house tour, the hospital drive, the doctor's *should*, and the question in the small room.

## 4.1 FOUR HUNDRED HOURS
**From:** Orla's old line *"Our day is four hundred hours. We are always tired."* **Where:** the ship; Glorietta
Bay off the marina's fuel dock; the east ring up to 10th St; home. **Giver:** the green dot. **Length:** 25 min.

**You do:** choose where a spaceship lands, talk it down, and walk two aliens home across a waking town.

**Beats**
1. **03:00.** The green light flickers. The hum is wrong. On the ship the floor leans and something is ringing.
   **Orla**, calm, the way people are calm in hospitals: *"The ship is tired. Four hundred hours. It must sleep.
   On the ground."* *"Where? You know the ground."*
2. **The glass floor again.** In 1.6 you named these places. Now you choose between them, and say why:
   - The ocean beach. **YOU:** *"Not there. There are people."*
   - The Ferry Landing. **YOU:** *"Not there. There is a police boat."*
   - The docks. **YOU:** *"Not there. There are lights."*
   - The bay beside the marina, deep and dark. **YOU:** *"There. It is quiet."*
   (A wrong choice is allowed. It makes the walk home longer and louder.)
3. **Down.** Orla flies badly when she is tired. You call it:
   **YOU:** *"Left. Left! Down. Slow! Stop. Down."* The ship goes into Glorietta Bay at four in the morning with
   no splash at all, and settles on the bottom. A green glow under the water, fading. The marina boats rock at
   their ropes.
4. **Ashore.** You swim. Zib floats on its back like a cork. Orla sinks, and walks along the bottom, and comes up
   the boat ramp with weed on her head.
5. **The walk home.** Seven hundred metres, uphill, before the sun. Glorietta Blvd, the ring park, the back
   streets to 10th. Zib in the jacket. Orla in a blue cover from somebody's boat. A town waking up:
   - a runner with headphones: stand still, be two people waiting for a bus;
   - a street sweeper: behind the parked cars, crouched;
   - a patrol car, slow, window down: **YOU:** *"Look!"* and a green blink three streets away turns it round;
   - a boy on a bike with newspapers, who sees everything and says *"Cool costume."* **Orla:** *"Thank you.
     It is my skin."* **YOU:** *"Orla. Quiet."*
6. **The hall.** {H}, in a dressing gown, with a glass of water, at the foot of the stairs. Two aliens in the
   doorway. A very long time.
   **YOU:** *"{H}. This is Orla. This is Zib."*
   **Orla**, who has heard this scene in a Report and practised it: *"Hi! You must be my new flatmate. Nice to
   meet you!"* `(H's own old lines)`
   **{H}**, from far away: *"...Welcome home. Make yourself... at home."* `(old words, slower)` Then: *"The jelly. The jelly
   was REAL."* From the kitchen sink: *"Blub."*
7. **Upstairs.** The attic studio. The forms come off the wall. The recorder goes in a box. Two aliens sit on the
   floor under the roof window.
   **{H}**, to you, at the door: *"That door is their room. It's locked. Please don't go in, OK?"* `(old, turned
   round)` And this week's report, if {H} still writes them, has two words in it: *"Nothing new."*

**Carries:** *there is / there are* with a reason; orders under pressure; introducing people.
**Gold:** the bay the first time; home before sunrise; seen by nobody but the paper boy.

## 4.2 CLASS TRIP
**From:** Grace's class (3.4); Goo. **Where:** home; Christ Church Community Center; Coronado University: the
Quad, the Science Building, the campus garage; 6th St. **Giver:** G. **Length:** 35 min.

**You do:** lose your translator, plan a break-in with a class of beginners, get into a lab as a cleaner, and get
out as a puddle.

**Beats**
1. **Morning.** A plumbers' van outside. Two men in clean overalls and good shoes. *"Water company. There's a
   problem with the drains."* Ten minutes later they carry the pipe from under the kitchen sink out to the van
   in a sealed box. Something in the box says *"Class is not over!"* The van has no company name on it.
2. **Silence.** The drop in your ear is cold and still. No small voice under the words. No tricks: say *"Stop!"*
   and nothing stops. For the first time since day thirteen the island is in English only.
   And you walk down Amber Avenue, and somebody asks you the time, and a bus announces a stop, and you
   understand them. Not all of it. Enough. (The game says nothing about this. The player notices, or does not.
   The officer's slow phone app from chapter 1 is still there for anyone who needs it.)
3. **Where is Goo?** Zib, in the attic, turns the dial on the old tape recorder until it hums green, and points
   it west. Then draws: a tall building, a square, a flag. **YOU:** *"The university."* Zib claps.
4. **The plan.** Tuesday, the hall, the board. Grace gives you the chalk. You have no trick, no translator and
   eleven people with a few hundred words between them. So the plan is made of small words, and everybody puts
   in the one thing they have:
   - **Mei:** *"Cleaners go everywhere. Nobody looks."* She knows who cleans the Science Building and when.
   - **Lina:** two grey cleaners' coats, from Shift Linen & Uniforms, taken in to fit by tonight.
   - **Omar:** the canteen's food trolley. *"Guards are hungry. Always."*
   - **Yusuf:** a bench on the Quad, a newspaper, a phone. *"I sit. I see. I call."*
   - **{F}:** the library's van, with a box of books for the University Library, parked in the campus garage.
   - **Sal:** *"I drive. Fast. Finally!"*
   - **Ivan:** *"Door."* He means: whichever door you come out of, he will be standing at it.
   - **Grace** writes it on the board as a lesson: *First. Then. After that. At ten o'clock.* It is the best
     class she has ever taught.
   - (If you said *yes* to Rhodes, this scene starts differently. They know. **Ivan:** *"We are friends?"* It is
     a question now. **YOU:** *"I was wrong. I am sorry."* A long look. Then he pulls out two chairs.)
5. **In.** Night, the Quad, the Science Building. You and Mei, in grey coats, with a mop bucket. No tricks. You
   get through by doing what you have done for thirty days: listening.
   - Two guards at the lift: *"Coffee at ten. Five minutes."* That is your window.
   - A scientist on a phone in the corridor: *"...third floor, the cold room, at the end."* That is your door.
   - A guard stops you: *"Hey. You're new."* **YOU:** *"Yes. I am new here."* `(old, from the taxi)` *"What's
     your name?"* Say it like someone who belongs. He holds the door for the bucket.
   - Yusuf, on the phone, very slowly: *"One. Man. Goes. Left."*
   - Omar's trolley arrives in the guard room exactly at ten. Nobody leaves it.
6. **The cold room.** A glass jar under a lamp. A Navy language expert is sitting in front of it, holding up
   cards: a cat, a house, a ball. Goo is not answering. Goo is teaching:
   **Goo:** *"No! Wrong! 'It is a ball.' Say it again. Class is not over!"*
   He has been in there for six hours. He looks grateful to see a cleaner.
   Goo has spent a day listening to scientists. Goo fills the jar.
7. **Out.** The jar cracks. Cold in your ear, and the small voice: *"Student! You came! You are late!"* And then:
   *"Say: PUDDLE."* **YOU:** *"Puddle."* The world goes flat and green and low. You and Goo go under the
   cold-room door, between two pairs of boots, under the lift doors, down. You stand up in the campus garage,
   whole, and nobody has ever felt worse.
8. **The run.** Alarms. Ivan is at the door, as promised, holding it shut from the outside with his back.
   Sal's taxi, the back door open. 6th St, east, two dark vans behind. The class is already on the route:
   - a food trolley rolls slowly across Union Plaza;
   - a rope of bed sheets comes down across a windscreen at the corner of C Avenue;
   - a book bike, ridden by a librarian, is suddenly in the middle of a junction;
   - **YOU:** *"Stop!"* *"Up!"* *"Fast!"* It all works again.
9. Home. Goo goes back in the sink and is quiet for almost a minute. Then: *"Tomorrow: school."*

**Carries:** *first, then, after that*; times; hearing a floor, a room, a time; everything, without help.
**Gold:** in and out inside the five minutes; never stopped twice; the vans lost before Amber Avenue.
**Opens:** the trick **Puddle.**

## 4.3 LIGHTS OUT
**From:** the camp's morning sport (P2); the hospital drive (2.4). **Where:** home to the marina by the east
ring; Glorietta Bay; under the bridge; round the whole island. **Giver:** nobody. It is day 38. **Length:**
25 min.

**You do:** talk your way through a checkpoint, then fly a spaceship that only listens to you.

**Beats**
1. **Evening.** Floodlights on Glorietta Bay. The harbour patrol boat off the fuel dock. Helicopters in a slow
   circle. The bridge is shut. Rhodes has found the glow. The ship has slept three days; it can fly tonight, or
   he will have it by morning.
2. **The drive.** {H}'s car, {H}'s hand healed, {H} at the wheel. Two shapes under a blanket in the back. You in
   the passenger seat, because you are the one who can read the road signs and hear the police radio.
   It is 2.4 the other way round. Now you give the directions, and you know what a bad passenger sounds like:
   **YOU:** *"Left here. Slow down. Stop: the light is red. Now go. The second street on the right."*
3. **The checkpoint.** Glorietta Blvd. One patrol car, one officer with a torch. This is the last talk-down, and
   it is all yours:
   **Officer:** *"Evening. Road's closed. Where are you going?"*
   **YOU:** *"Good evening, officer. We are going to the marina. My friend has a boat."*
   *"Who's in the back?"* **YOU:** *"My friends. They are tourists. They are very tired."*
   **Orla**, from under the blanket: *"We are always tired."* `(old)`
   *"ID?"* You hold out a licence and a release paper with two stamps. He reads the paper for a long time. With
   no strikes he says *"Mind how you go,"* and lifts the tape. With two, he walks round the car first.
4. **The dock.** The ship comes up out of the black water beside the fuel dock, dripping, dark. Rhodes on a loud
   speaker from the patrol boat, as fast as ever. Inside, the board is full of green and none of it is right.
   **Orla:** *"The ship listens to English. Tonight the air is full of English. Navy English. It hears them. It
   does not hear us."* She looks at you. *"Your voice it knows. You talk to it every night."*
5. **The flight.** Your voice and your thumbs. Every order is one the camp guard gave you on a cold morning:
   - **YOU:** *"Up!"* Off the water. **YOU:** *"Lights off!"* The bay goes dark under you.
   - **YOU:** *"Forward! Fast!"* The patrol boat turns too late.
   - The bridge, shut, lit, full of blue lights. **YOU:** *"Down! Under!"* Between the legs of the bridge, a
     metre over the water.
   - Two helicopters. **YOU:** *"Left! Right! Up! Stop!"* They go past. **YOU:** *"Go!"*
   - And then round the island, the whole of it, once, low and fast, because the only clear way out is the long
     way: the east beaches, the hospital and its helipad, the Ferry Landing, the steel works chimney (**"Over!"**),
     the base fence with a yard and tents and a board behind it, very small. The stadium. (**Zib** points.
     **YOU:** *"Yes. The hat."*) The hotel's red roof, the old lookout tower, and up the hill over 10th Street,
     where a kitchen light is on.
6. **The radio.** Rhodes tries the ship himself. *"Vessel, you are ordered to land, bring it down nice and easy
   and nobody gets hurt."* The ship does nothing.
   **Orla**, on the radio, slowly, kindly: *"It cannot hear you. You speak too fast."*
   A long silence. Then, for the second time in his life: **Rhodes:** *"Please. Come. Down."*
   The ship slows. It heard that. It begins to turn.
   **YOU:** *"Up."*
   It knows your voice. It goes up.
7. Cloud. Then no cloud. The island is a small bright shape on a black sea, with a bridge like a thread. The
   board is calm. Zib's clock on the wall says **DAY 39**.

**Carries:** the first ten words you learned, as fast as you can say them; directions; staying calm and clear with an
officer.
**Gold:** through the checkpoint in one go; under the bridge without a touch; never caught in a light.

## 4.4 WHAT SHOULD WE DO NOW?
**From:** ch8, *"What should I do now?"* **Where:** the ship, above the island; your roof. **Giver:** Orla.
**Length:** 8 min.

**You do:** explain one word, and give the advice that ends the story.

**Beats**
1. Quiet. Orla has your file on the wall again, the photo and the stamp.
   **Orla:** *"Tomorrow they ask: who are you. And it says here: ALIEN. Now you have many words. Tell us."*
   In 1.6 you had no way to say it. Now the lines are there:
   **YOU:** *"Alien is a word on a paper."*
   **YOU:** *"It means: a person from another place."*
   **YOU:** *"I am from another place. You are from another place."*
   **Orla:** *"So we are the same."* **YOU:** *"Yes."*
   **Orla:** *"Then why do they have a different word for you?"*
   **YOU:** *"Next question, please."* `(old)`
   Zib laughs. It is the first sound Zib has made.
2. **Orla:** *"We cannot land again. He knows the bay."* She turns from the window and asks it the way {H} asked
   it on a sofa with a bandaged hand: *"What should we do now?"* `(old, with "we")`
   - **YOU:** *"You should go home."* They go. It is forty light years. **Orla:** *"You're right. We should go."*
     Goo is asked, and chooses the sink.
   - **YOU:** *"You should stay."* The attic, the locked door, the jacket and the hat. **Orla:** *"OK. We should
     be quiet."* She will not be.
   - **YOU:** *"You should ask."* The slow way. The papers. *"I tried legal ways, but I was rejected."* `(old)`
     **YOU:** *"It is slow. You should ask anyway."* **Orla:** *"Zib will write the form. Zib writes very well."*
3. They set you down on your own roof at first light, by the attic window. **Orla:** *"Good human."* `(old)`
   **YOU:** *"Not 'human'. {NAME}."* **Orla:** *"Good {NAME}. Sleep well!"* `(old words)`

**Carries:** *it means ...*; *should*, as a real choice.
**Gold:** none. Nobody gets a medal for this one.

## 4.5 WHO ARE YOU?
**From:** the interrogation room (P3); the gate officer's *"I really hope you make it."* **Where:** Island Legal
Aid; Coronado County Courthouse. **Giver:** O. **Length:** 15 min.

**You do:** answer the first question of the game.

**Beats**
1. **Day 40.** Island Legal Aid, a small office by the courthouse. A young lawyer with too many folders. *"I can
   stand next to you. I can't talk for you. The judge wants to hear you."*
2. **The courtroom.** The judge. Reyes, with a folder and a paper that has two stamps. Rhodes, with photographs.
   Behind you, the benches are full, and how full depends on what you did in forty days.
3. **Rhodes** goes first, standing, quick and friendly: bay, lights, unidentified, persons of interest, a matter
   of national...
   **Judge:** *"Commander. Slowly, please."* `(your line, from the first ten minutes of the game)`
   He tries. Said slowly, in plain words, with everybody looking, it is this: **Rhodes:** *"There was... a
   green jelly. In a kitchen sink. It gave my man a lesson."* Somebody laughs. Then the room.
   His photographs: a bus. A car park. A child in a hat on a beach. A tall woman at a football game.
   **Judge:** *"Thank you, Commander."*
4. **The reports.** Reyes: *"The host's reports, Your Honour."* {H} stands up at the back. *"Can I read the last
   one?"* It is a new sheet, and it is your save file, in {H}'s words:
   *"Day 10. Likes pizza. Day 11. Got a job. Day 13. Lost it. Day 14. Got another one. Drove me to hospital.
   Had no licence. Got one. Day 24. Paid the rent. Washes the dishes. Now. English: five. This is my friend."*
5. **The benches.** The judge asks if anyone will speak. One line each, from whoever you earned:
   **Sal:** *"Not a sailor. Better."* **Ms. Bell:** *"On time. Every day."* **Grace:** *"A good student. A
   better teacher."* **Ivan**, standing, enormous: *"Friend."* **Mr. Dunn**, at the back, surprised to be
   there: *"Late once. Once."* Omar, Lina, Yusuf, Mei, Sam, {F}.
6. **The question.** **Judge:** *"Now you. Who are you?"*
   The screen fills with your own forty days in pieces: your name, your age, where you live, the job you had at
   home and the two you had here, what you can do, what you like, who your friends are, what you did, what you
   are going to do. Put together five sentences, or eight, or twelve. Say them. There is no wrong answer and
   nobody is counting.
   When you finish, the game plays it back, in your voice, over the small room from the first hour: the metal
   desk, the tired officer, the silence where your answer should have been.
7. The judge reads the file. *"Status..."* The stamp. **DAY 40.**
   - No strikes, or one: *"Resident."*
   - Two, or three: *"Thirty more days. Then we talk again."* (The game goes on. The clock comes back. It is
     gentler.)
8. The steps outside. **Reyes:** *"You made it."* `(old: "I really hope you make it.")`
   Sal's taxi at the kerb. **Sal:** *"Where to?"* `(old)` **YOU:** *"Home."*
   **Sal:** *"On Uncle Sam?"* **YOU:** *"No. This one is on me."* `(old gold line)`

**Carries:** everything. It is the only test in the game, and the player wrote the questions.
**Gold:** a full bench.

# EPILOGUE: DAY 41

## E1. QUICK QUESTION
**Where:** home. **Length:** 5 min, then for as long as you like.

1. Morning. No day number in the corner of the screen.
2. The kitchen. Goo in the sink, the size of a cat, with a certificate of its own on the fridge next to yours.
   {H} at the cooker, two hands working.
   **{H}:** *"Quick question!"* `(old)` You wait for it.
   **{H}:** *"So, what do you want? Pizza or tacos?"* `(old)`
3. What the choice in 4.4 left behind:
   - **Go home:** every third night, at three, for one second, a green light crosses your ceiling. Nothing else.
   - **Stay:** two more for dinner. The attic door is never quite shut. Orla wants a job. The Hum meter is yours
     for good, and so are the field trips.
   - **Ask:** a week later Reyes rings. A parcel has come to the Release Desk from very far away: an application,
     four hundred pages, in perfect handwriting. *"I'm going to need a bigger folder."*
4. The island is open. Your old job, the one you told Sal about on day ten, is on the Jobs page again. It no
   longer says *"Not yet."*

**After the story:** everything in OPEN_WORLD.md, the class on Tuesdays, the rent every ten days, and the room
upstairs for whatever comes next (the A2 story).
