# ALIEN STATUS: missions, part 1 (Prologue and Chapter 1)

How to read a mission:
- **From** = the old game's scene it adapts. **Where** = the place on the new map.
- **You do** = the play. **Beats** = the scene in order, with the key lines.
- `(old)` after a line = kept word for word from the old game's `strings.csv` (already translated, often already
  recorded). `{NAME}`, `{H}` (the housemate: Emma or Jack), `{F}` (the friend: Mia or Leo) are filled in by the game.
- **YOU:** lines are said into the microphone (or typed, or tapped).
- **Carries** = what the mission quietly practises. Never shown to the player.
- **Gold** = the gold medal.
