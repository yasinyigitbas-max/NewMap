# ALIEN STATUS: missions, part 1 (Prologue and Chapter 1)

Version 2 (GTA rework). How to read a mission:
- **From** = the old game's scene it adapts. **Where** = the place on the new map.
- **CUT** = a cutscene. Its number is its row in `CUTSCENES.md`, which names the clips. Every cutscene has a bird
  somewhere in the frame; it is only pointed out here when it matters.
- **PLAY** = you have the controls.
- `(old)` after a line = word for word from the old game's `strings.csv`. `(old pattern)` = an old line with its
  blank filled in. `{NAME}`, `{H}` (the housemate: Emma or Jack), `{F}` (the friend: Mia or Leo).
- **YOU:** lines are said into the microphone (or typed, or tapped).
- **Carries** = what the mission quietly practises. Never shown. **Gold** = the gold medal.

## P2. RAN FOR IT
**From:** the gap between sketch 4 and sketch 5 (*"They caught us."*). **Where:** the container depot, Bayline's
truck court and the base fence on Alameda Blvd (industrial docks), at night. **Length:** 8 min.

**You do:** run. This is the whole movement system in eight minutes, with nobody explaining it.

- **CUT P2-1 "Over the Side"** (40 s). The black side of a ship. A rope net. Twenty people coming down it onto a
  concrete quay between walls of containers, bags held in their teeth. Nobody speaks. A gull on a mooring post
  turns its head to watch each one land. You are the ninth. A torch comes on, far down the quay. A whistle. A
  voice through a loudspeaker, in a language you do not have: **"STOP! STAY WHERE YOU ARE!"** Everybody runs.
- **PLAY.** One small word on the screen each time, with its Turkish: **RUN. OVER. UNDER. UP. JUMP. HOLD. WAIT.**
  - **Run** down a lane of containers. A concrete barrier: **over** it (vault) without slowing.
  - A gap between two containers a shoulder wide: turn sideways and go **through** (squeeze). The man behind you
    is too big, and tears a gate off its post instead. You will know him later.
  - A stack two high: run at it and **up** (running wall climb, mantle). Along the tops. A gap of three metres
    to the next stack: **jump**. You hang by your hands (grab) and pull up.
  - A searchlight crosses the roofs. **Wait**, flat, in the shadow of a container, while it goes over (cover).
  - Down: hang from the edge and **drop**. A woman drops her bag as she lands, and does not stop for it. You can
    pick it up, or not.
  - The truck court. Guards with torches between the trailers. **Under** a trailer on your stomach. A man is
    already under there. He gives you his hand and pulls you through. *"Up! Up!"* It is the only English he has.
  - A warehouse wall along the water with a ledge the width of a foot: back to the wall, **hold**, and edge along
    it over the black bay (ledge sidle). A pipe across to the next roof: walk it (beam).
  - The last fence. Wire, three metres, and on the other side a street with palm trees and a traffic light
    changing for nobody. Run at it, **up**, hands on the top.
  - The voices behind you all the way are the first English you ever hear: **"Stop!" "Get down!" "Don't move!"
    "Hands up!"**
- **CUT P2-2 "They Caught Us"** (30 s). Your hands on the top wire. The street, the palms, the green light. A
  hand closes on your ankle. The fence going past upward. Gravel. Boots. Torch beams crossing. The others in a
  line on their knees with their hands on their heads: the big one, the woman without her bag, the man from under
  the trailer. On the fence above them, the gull. The picture turns to ink:

**Carries:** *run, over, under, up, jump, wait*; and four orders you will give back later.
**Gold:** never caught in the light; the bag picked up; under four minutes.
