# ALIEN STATUS: missions, part 3 (Chapter 3: The Rent, days 21 to 30)

Version 2 (GTA rework). Read `MISSIONS_1_PROLOGUE_CH1.md` first for how a mission is written. **CUT** = a
cutscene (clips in `CUTSCENES.md`). **PLAY** = you have the controls. `(old)` = the old game's line, word for
word.

Everything from here is new, grown from what the old game left open: Zib's jacket, Sal's *"next time you pay"*,
Ivan's *"we are friends?"*, Grace's *"maybe we will meet again"*, the rent *"in two weeks"*, the officer's tests.

**The chapter is open.** 3.1 to 3.4 and W3 are strands and can be played in any order. 3.5 opens on day 23 when
two strands are done, and leads into Vince's missions (3.6, 3.7, 3.9, 3.10). 3.8 is a ship night. 3.11 is day 30.

## 3.1 ZIB NEEDS A JACKET
**From:** Orla's old line *"Cold! Terrible. Zib needs a jacket."* **Where:** Second Hand Rose and Crownado Vintage
(gritty edge); Amber Ave down to Amber Beach Plaza. **Giver:** the green dot. **Length:** 25 min.

**You do:** shop for someone with no feet, walk a silent alien through town on a lead made of words, and hold a
beach against the Navy for ninety seconds.

- **CUT 3.1-1 "One Day"** (30 s). Ship night. **Orla:** *"Zib wants to see it. One day. On the ground."* Zib nods
  so hard the ship moves. *"Zib needs Earth clothes."* She pays you with a twenty the ship made. It is perfect.
  So are the other fifty. They all have the same number. Then, at Orla speed, once: *"A big jacket. Green is
  good. A hat. Dark glasses. No shoes. Zib has no feet."*
- **PLAY: the shops.** No list unless you ask. **YOU:** *"Can I have a big green jacket, please?"* *"It is too
  small."* *"It is too big."* *"How much is it?"* A dummy the size of Zib stands in the corner. Nice words get a
  better price.
- **CUT 3.1-2 "Tourist"** (25 s). Dawn, the vacant lot. A beam, a hum, and Zib is standing in the weeds: one
  metre twenty, grey, six fingers. You hold out the jacket. Zib puts it on backwards. You fix it. Hat. Glasses.
  Zib takes out a notebook and looks at you, ready.
- **PLAY: the walk.** Down Amber to the sea. Zib follows you, more or less, copies what people do and touches
  what Zib likes. Your voice is the lead: **YOU:** *"Zib, wait."* *"Zib, come here!"* *"Don't touch!"* *"Stop!"*
  *"No. Not that."* *"OK. Go."*
  - The crossing: the red hand. **YOU:** *"Wait."* ... *"Go."*
  - Hilltop Market: Zib eats an apple, sticker first. **YOU:** *"Sorry! How much is it?"*
  - A police officer: *"Good morning."* Zib waves, with all six. **YOU:** *"Look!"* (a new word: a green blink
    on the church spire; he looks up; you are round the corner).
  - A stranger: a man selling watches from his coat stops Zib. Zib wants all of them.
  - A child: *"You look funny."* Zib writes it down, delighted.
  - The bus. Zib gets on alone and will not get off. You ride the loop twice. Driver: *"Is that your kid?"*
    **YOU:** *"Yes."*
- **CUT 3.1-3 "The Sea"** (30 s). Amber Beach Plaza, the old lookout tower. Zib walks to the edge of the water
  and stops. Zib has seen this ocean from forty light years and from forty kilometres. Never from the sand. The
  glasses come off. For once Zib does not write. On the tower rail, six gulls in a row, all looking the same
  way. Then a jogger's phone comes up, and far down the beach, a white van turns onto the sand.
- **PLAY: ninety seconds.** The beam needs ninety seconds to find Zib. Men in white come up the beach on foot
  from two vans: nets, stun rifles. Keep them off: the Puddler, the Stopper on the vans, lifeguard towers and
  beach walls for cover. The Hum meter is on the screen for the first time, and full.
- **CUT 3.1-4 "Earth Is Very Good"** (25 s). The beam takes Zib up off the wet sand, hat last. A helicopter comes
  down the beach with its light on, over a row of green puddles and you, walking away with your hands in your
  pockets. That night **Orla** reads from the notebook: *"'Wait.' 'Don't touch.' 'You look funny.' 'Is that your
  kid.' Zib says Earth is very good."*

**Carries:** clothes, colours, sizes, *too big / too small*, prices; *don't*.
**Gold:** Orla's list from memory; nothing stolen; nobody reaches Zib.
**Opens:** **"Look!"**; the Hum meter; photo homework.

## 3.2 NEXT TIME YOU PAY
**From:** Sal's *"...but next time you pay"*; the doctor's *"You should get a licence"*; the old driving
instructor. **Where:** the Ferry Landing taxi bay; the stadium's Tailgate Lot; the city centre; Amber Ave to
Hotel Bel. **Giver:** S. **Length:** 25 min.

**You do:** learn to drive from a man who talks too fast, pass a test given by voice alone, and win your first
car race.

- **CUT 3.2-1 "No Licence?"** (20 s). The phone, one breath: **Sal:** *"Youdrovetothehospital? Nolicence?
  Buddy!"* **YOU:** *"Sal. Slowly, please."* **Sal:** *"You. Drove. No. Licence. Come to the ferry. Now."*
- **PLAY: the Tailgate Lot.** Sal's cones. His taxi has a second brake on his side and he uses it. *"Forward!
  Slow. Slower! Stop. Back. Left, left, left. Good! Park between the cones."* When he gets excited the words run
  together and you say it again: *"Slowly, please."* (If your CV said you can drive: five cones, and *"OK, OK,
  you are good, I see it."*)
- **PLAY: the test.** An examiner with a clipboard in the back. No map, no arrow, no pins. What she says, once
  each: *"Turn left at the next junction." "Take the second street on the right." "Stop behind the white van."
  "Park in front of the bakery." "Go straight on at the lights."* Round the city centre. A wrong turn is a
  mark. Six is a fail; come back tomorrow. **Examiner:** *"Stop here, please. ... You passed."* A licence, with
  your camp photo on it.
- **CUT 3.2-2 "Island Cab"** (35 s). The Ferry Landing bay. A newer, shinier taxi has parked across Sal's place.
  Its driver, young, sunglasses, leaning on the door: fast and unkind, most of it about Sal's age and Sal's car.
  **Goo:** *"He says... old. Slow. Museum."* Sal goes quiet. Then he gives you the keys. *"Hotel Bel. First one
  there. You drive. I talk."*
- **PLAY: the race.** Two taxis, the Ferry Landing to Hotel Bel, the length of the island. Sal is your co-driver
  and for once every word is slow, clear and exactly on time: *"Left! Now right. Straight on. Bus! Go round.
  Hard left at the church!"* Follow his calls and you carry your speed. Any route counts. So does **"Stop!"** on
  the other cab, if you do not mind the green.
- **CUT 3.2-3 "This One Is on Me"** (30 s). Hotel Bel's drive, a red roof, a gull on the taxi sign. The young
  driver arrives second and says nothing at all. A sailor with a bag gets into your back seat: **YOU:** *"Where
  to?"* `(old gold line)` Later, the fare in your hand, you hold it out to Sal. **YOU:** *"The gate. Day ten. It
  was on Uncle Sam."* **Sal:** *"Ha! You remember!"* **YOU:** *"This one is on me."* `(old gold line)` He takes
  one dollar.

**Carries:** *turn left at, the second on the right, behind, in front of, between, straight on*; calls at speed.
**Gold:** no marks on the test; first to the hotel without the Stopper.
**Opens:** Coronado Cab; taxi races; **"Come here!"** (calls your car or bike).

## 3.3 FIFTY WORDS
**From:** Ivan, camp day 9. **Where:** Big Al's Tires, the car pound, the vacant lot (gritty edge). **Giver:** I.
**Length:** 25 min.

**You do:** take a friend's car back out of a crooked pound, at night, with a partner who knows fifty words.

- **CUT 3.3-1 "Friend?"** (35 s). A message: a photo of two orange camp shoes and one word. **FRIEND?** Big Al's
  Tires, the night shift. Ivan fills the doorway. *"OK, OK! We are friends!"* He lifts you off the ground. Then
  he shows you a paper, as if you were a lawyer. He bought a car with his first pay. It was parked outside his
  own door. Now it is behind a fence across the road, and the paper says **$300**, and at the top it says
  **MARLOW RECOVERY. A Payday Loans 4U company.** He has forty dollars. *"Night. Fence. Car. Go."*
- **PLAY: two ways.**
  - **Pay.** The pound's window, in the morning, $300 of your own. A man behind glass says *"Sign here, here and
    here"* without looking up. Read the form for Ivan and fill it in. Clean, dear, and you have met Vince's
    business.
  - **Night.** You are the eyes and the reader; Ivan is the strength, and does exactly what you say, at once:
    **YOU:** *"Wait."* *"Go."* *"Down!"* *"Up."* *"Over."* *"Under."* *"Quiet!"* *"Behind the van."*
    One guard with a torch. A camera on a pole. Ivan lifts the bottom of the fence with one hand; you go under;
    **YOU:** *"Open!"* at the gate from inside. **"Look!"** sends the torch the wrong way. If Ivan is seen he
    stands very still and says *"Hello, my friend!"* `(Omar's old line: the only greeting he has)`. It works
    once.
- **CUT 3.3-2 "Up"** (25 s). A yellow clamp on the wheel. Ivan looks at you. Something silver drops out of your
  ear into your hand: tonight's present. **Goo:** *"Say: UP."* **YOU:** *"Up!"* The car lifts a metre off the
  ground, turning slowly, green underneath. The clamp hangs in the air under it and falls off. Ivan crosses
  himself. Lights come on in the pound office.
- **PLAY: out.** Two of Marlow's tow pickups and four men with bats. The Lifter throws what is lying around:
  tyres, a bin, a clamp. Then drive: no lights, the alleys of the gritty edge, Ivan in the passenger seat
  because he bought a car before he learned to drive one, pointing and shouting the only directions he knows:
  *"Go! Go! Left! NO! Go!"*
- **CUT 3.3-3 "Friends"** (25 s). The vacant lot. The car ticks. **Ivan:** *"You. Me. Friends. Yes?"* **YOU:**
  *"We are friends."* `(old)` He opens the boot: a wrench, a bat, a board with writing on it. **A RED PICKUP. A
  WHITE VAN. A BLUE TAXI.** He taps the board, then the garage, then rubs his fingers together.

**Carries:** *wait, go, up, down, over, under, behind, quiet*; colours and vehicles; reading a form.
**Gold:** never seen before the clamp; nobody's car scratched but theirs.
**Opens:** the **Lifter**; Ivan's list; night races; the vacant lot fights; *Call Ivan*.

## 3.4 MAYBE WE WILL MEET AGAIN
**From:** Grace's goodbye; camp days 4 and 7. **Where:** Christ Church Community Center; the docks, Adela Wash &
Fold, the hospital, Tenth Street Park. **Giver:** G. **Length:** 30 min, in pieces.

**You do:** find five people across the island, fix what is stopping each of them, win a fight in a laundry, and
teach a class.

- **CUT 3.4-1 "Bring a Friend"** (40 s). The book comes back to the library with a note in a hand you know from
  a board in a yard: *"Thank you. Tuesday at seven. Bring a friend."* The hall. Chairs in a circle. One old man
  asleep. **Grace:** *"Hello! I am Grace. I am your teacher."* `(old)` She says it the same way. Then she laughs.
  She looks at your ear. *"What is that?"* **YOU:** *"A teacher."* *"Is it good?"* **YOU:** *"No."* **Goo:**
  *"BLUB!"* A letter from the city on her table: fewer than ten students by Friday and the hall goes to a yoga
  class. **Grace:** *"They are all here. On the island. They are tired. They work. They are afraid."*
- **PLAY: five names.**
  - **Omar**, the Shift Canteen in the docks. His line does not close until seven. Twenty minutes on the serving
    line beside him: one round of the old job minigame on canteen words (**beat shift**: say each order as it
    crosses the line).
  - **Mei**, the Atrium Cafe in the hospital. *"I am very tired."* `(old)` She gets on the bus and falls asleep
    on your shoulder. The stops are called out loud. Hear yours, and wake her.
  - **Yusuf** is the old man asleep in the hall. In the day he is in Tenth Street Park with a board and a stone
    for one pawn. If you still have the bottle top from the camp: **YOU:** *"This is your pawn."*
  - **Ivan** comes if 3.3 is done. He takes two chairs.
  - **Lina**, Adela Wash & Fold.
- **CUT 3.4-2 "Insurance"** (30 s). The laundromat. Lina behind the counter, not moving. Two men, one sitting on
  a washing machine eating her lunch, one counting what is in her till. A bat lies across the folding table.
  The one with the till, friendly, fast: something about *insurance*, *this month*, *Uncle Vince*. He sees you.
  *"We're closed, pal."*
- **PLAY: the laundry.** A fist fight between rows of machines. Things to use: an iron, a basket, a machine
  door, a cloud of soap powder. The Puddler, if you want one of them to go down a floor drain. Lina hits the
  second one with a wet sheet.
  Then forty ironed shirts still have to reach the Bayview Inn before she can leave: the book bike, a pile
  higher than your head.
- **CUT 3.4-3 "Now You Teach"** (50 s). Friday. Eleven people in a circle, most of whom you fed, carried, woke
  up or fought for. The city's clipboard goes round. **Grace:** *"Good. Now you teach. Tell them how to take the
  bus."* The chairs become a bus; you are the driver. **YOU:** *"Good morning! Where to?"* *"One ticket,
  please."* *"Sit down, please."* *"This is your stop."* They copy you. Ivan gets it wrong, everyone laughs, and
  he laughs. After, stacking chairs: **Grace:** *"The app on your phone. That is my voice. I made it. It is not
  a teacher. People are teachers."* *"In this world, you can talk to every person."* `(old)` And, at the door,
  quietly: *"All of them owe that man. All of them signed."*

**Carries:** inviting; days and times; hearing a stop name; teaching what you know.
**Gold:** all five on Friday; Mei woken at the right stop; the shirts clean.
**Opens:** class nights; the Community Kitchen club; the camp four's stories.

## W3. THE NEST
**From:** new. **Where:** Harbor Steel (industrial): the yard, the mill wall, the water tower. **Giver:** W.
**Opens:** after 3.1. **Length:** 20 min.

**You do:** the longest climb in the game, a walk through a thousand sleeping birds, and the run back down.

- **CUT W3-1 "Where They Sleep"** (35 s). The van, night. Finch has a new photograph, long lens, grainy: the
  steel works' water tower against the moon, and its top is the wrong shape. **Finch:** *"Every night at ten.
  All of them. All of them. They go up there. And they stand. They don't sleep. They stand."* He taps the
  middle of the picture: a small green light. *"I am sixty-eight. I can't climb that. You can."*
- **PLAY: the climb.** Up the outside of Harbor Steel, every move the game has: a run up the yard wall, a pipe
  across a gap (beam), a ledge along the mill's brick face with your back to it (sidle), a drainpipe (grab and
  climb), a jump to the tower's leg, the ladder with a missing section (hang, swing, gap). The island gets
  smaller and brighter below you.
- **PLAY: the nest.** The tank's roof. A thousand birds, gulls and pigeons and crows together, standing in rows,
  all facing in, eyes open. In the middle, something the size of a football, humming green. Walk slowly. Do not
  touch one. They are spaced exactly one step apart.
- **CUT W3-2 "They Don't Sleep"** (20 s). Your hand closes on the green thing. The hum stops. A thousand heads
  turn to you at once.
- **PLAY: down.** The horror game *The Wire* (OPEN_WORLD.md), for real and at height: the works' lights go out
  one by one behind you, and what is behind you is wings. Back down the way you came, faster: drop, slide,
  jump. The last light is over Finch's van door.
- **CUT W3-3 "They Are Ours"** (50 s). The van. The green thing on his table, his radar screen showing a thousand
  dots, all pointing at it. Finch is not triumphant. He sits down. *"Thirty years. They said: Walter, you're
  tired. Walter, see a doctor."* That night, the ship. You put the thing on the glass floor. **Orla** looks at
  it, and at Zib, and at you. *"Yes. They are ours. How do you think we know everything about you?"* Zib holds
  up a small bird and makes it wave. **Orla:** *"Now they are yours too."*

**Carries:** nothing. It is a climb and a fright.
**Gold:** to the top without a fall; through the nest without waking one early; down inside ninety seconds.
**Opens:** **bird view** (look at the street ahead from any bird); Finch's last mission in chapter 4.

## 3.5 THE RENT
**From:** *"The rent is in two weeks!"* **Where:** home; Little Frenchy. **Giver:** H. **Opens:** day 23.
**Length:** 25 min.

**You do:** work a Friday dinner rush as someone else's hands, and sign something you cannot read.

- **CUT 3.5-1 "Can You Cook?"** (30 s). Day 23. The kitchen at home. Two envelopes on the table, one thin.
  **{H}** holds up the bandage. *"I can't hold a knife. If I don't work tonight, I lose the job."* A look up at
  the ceiling. *"Can you cook?"* **YOU:** *"Yes, I can."* / *"No, I can't."* **{H}:** *"It doesn't matter. I
  talk. You do."*
- **PLAY: the rush.** Little Frenchy's kitchen. The chef calls each ticket once, loud and fast: *"Two soup, one
  fish, one steak, no salt on the steak! Go!"* {H} stands at your shoulder and says it again, small and clear:
  *"Two soups. One fish. One steak. No salt."* For the first time, {H} is working for you. The shift is the old
  job minigames on kitchen words, and they fit as if they were made for it:
  - **triage:** three tickets with urgency bars. Tap them in order, then say it: *"First I will plate the fish,
    then I will cut the bread, and finally I will wash the pan."* `(old pattern)`
  - **beat shift:** orders fall in three lanes; say each as it crosses the pass.
  Between rounds: a plate slides off the pass (**"Stop!"**, and you take it out of the air), and a pan catches
  fire.
- **CUT 3.5-2 "Uncle Vince"** (60 s). Midnight. Tips on the steel counter: enough for the rent, just. The back
  door opens without a knock. A big friendly man in a good coat, two others behind him. He hugs {H}, who goes
  stiff. **Vince:** *"Emma! Look at you, working hard, I love to see it. And this is the new one? From the
  camp? Welcome, welcome. Call me Uncle Vince."* He picks the tips up off the counter and counts them without
  looking at them. *"That's the interest."* **{H}**, very small: *"Two thousand. I borrowed two hundred."*
  Vince, fast, warm, a hand on your shoulder: something about *opportunity*, *family*, *work it off together*,
  *a little driving*. A paper. A pen, already out. *"Sign here, here and here."*
  **Goo:** *"It is very small. It says... 'the undersigned'... 'in perpetuity'... blub. I think it says
  'forever'."*
- **PLAY: sign.** You can ask (**YOU:** *"Slowly, please."*). He says it again, faster, smiling. You can refuse:
  the two men step forward, and it is a fight in a kitchen with frying pans, which you win, and tomorrow there
  are six of them at the house. One way or the other the story needs your name on that paper, and gets it.

**Carries:** food, numbers, *how many / how much*, *first ... then ... finally*; small print.
**Gold:** no plate sent back; both minigames flawless.
**Opens:** kitchen shifts; **$** on the map.

## 3.7 JUST DRIVE

**You do:** wait outside a bank with the engine running, and then drive faster than you ever have.

  more in the back, putting on gloves. **Vince**, on the phone, fast: a *pick-up*, a *withdrawal*, *in and out*,
  *keep the engine running*. **Goo:** *"He says: a delivery. From the bank. I think. He says 'withdrawal' many
  times."* They get out. They pull something down over their faces. **YOU:** *"...Not a delivery."* On the

  shouts directions that are wrong. A new present from the aliens fell out of your ear this morning: the
  **Hummer**. **YOU:** *"Go away!"* and a patrol car slides sideways across two lanes.

- **CUT 3.7-2 "Your Share"** (30 s). The pound, the gate shut behind you. Vince opens a bag, takes out one note,
  and puts it in your shirt pocket. *"Your share. The rest comes off what you owe. Which is now..."* He looks at
  the ceiling. *"...more. The van got scratched. Clause seven."*

**Carries:** *you can't park here*; excuses; directions at speed (and knowing when they are wrong).
**Gold:** no road block hit; clean on the first wash; the van without a mark.
**Opens:** the **Hummer**.
