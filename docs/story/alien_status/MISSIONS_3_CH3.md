# ALIEN STATUS: missions, part 3 (Chapter 3: The Rent, days 21 to 30)

Read `MISSIONS_1_PROLOGUE_CH1.md` first for how a mission is written. `(old)` = the old game's line, word for word.

Everything from here is new, and all of it grows from something the old game left open: Zib's jacket, Sal's *"next
time you pay"*, Ivan's *"we are friends?"*, Grace's *"maybe we will meet again"*, the rent *"in two weeks"*, the
officer's tests.

**The chapter is open.** 3.1 to 3.4 are four strands and can be played in any order. 3.5 opens on day 23, when two
of them are done. 3.6 is the next ship night after 3.5. 3.7 is day 30.

---

## 3.1 ZIB NEEDS A JACKET
**From:** Orla's old line *"Cold! Terrible. Zib needs a jacket."* **Where:** Second Hand Rose and Crownado Vintage
(gritty edge); Amber Ave from the city centre down to Amber Beach Plaza. **Giver:** the green dot. **Length:**
25 min.

**You do:** shop for someone with no feet, then walk a silent, curious alien through a busy town with your voice
as the lead.

**Beats**
1. **Ship night.** **Orla:** *"Zib wants to see it. One day. On the ground."* Zib nods so hard the ship moves.
   *"Zib needs Earth clothes. You know clothes."* She pays you with a twenty-dollar note the ship made. It is
   perfect. So are the other fifty. They all have the same number. Do not spend them.
2. **The list.** Orla says it once, at Orla speed: *"A big jacket. Green is good. A hat. Dark glasses. No shoes.
   Zib has no feet."* No list on the screen unless you ask for it.
3. **The shops.** At Second Hand Rose and Crownado Vintage, to real shop people:
   **YOU:** *"Can I have a big green jacket, please?"* **YOU:** *"It is too small."* *"It is too big."* **YOU:**
   *"How much is it?"* There is a dummy in the corner the size of Zib. Bargain a little: nice words get a better
   price (*"It's beautiful. But I only have twenty dollars."*). Buy with your own money.
4. **Dawn, the vacant lot.** A beam, a hum, and Zib is standing in the weeds: one metre twenty, grey, six fingers.
   Jacket. Hat. Glasses. Zib now looks like a small strange tourist. Zib has a notebook.
5. **The walk.** Down Amber Avenue to the sea. Zib follows you, more or less. Zib copies what people do, touches
   what Zib likes and writes down everything. Your voice is the lead:
   **YOU:** *"Zib, wait."* *"Zib, come here!"* *"Don't touch!"* *"Stop!"* *"No. Not that."* *"OK. Go."*
   What happens on the way:
   - **The crossing.** The red hand. Everybody waits. Zib does not know why. **YOU:** *"Wait."* ... *"Go."*
   - **The fruit.** Outside Hilltop Market Zib takes an apple and eats the sticker. **YOU:** *"Sorry! How much
     is it?"* You pay.
   - **The police officer.** *"Good morning."* Zib waves, with all six. **YOU:** *"Look!"* (the new trick: a
     green light blinks on the church spire, the officer looks up, and you are round the corner).
   - **The child.** *"You look funny."* Zib is delighted and writes it down.
   - **The bus.** Zib gets on a bus by itself. Zib loves the bus. You ride the whole loop twice because Zib will
     not get off. **YOU:** *"Zib. Come here. Please."* The driver: *"Is that your kid?"* **YOU:** *"Yes."*
6. **The beach.** Amber Beach Plaza, the old lookout tower. Zib has seen the ocean from forty light years and from
   forty kilometres. Never from the sand. Zib takes off the glasses. For the first time Zib does not write.
7. **Dusk.** The beam takes Zib up from the water's edge. A jogger's phone is pointing at it. The **Hum
   meter** shows for the first time, and fills. Thirty seconds later a Navy helicopter comes down the beach with
   its light on, and you learn to walk away from green the way you walk away from the police.
8. That night, the Report. **Orla** reads from Zib's notebook: *"'Wait.' 'Don't touch.' 'You look funny.' 'Is
   that your kid.' Zib says Earth is very good."*

**Carries:** clothes, colours, sizes, *too big / too small*, prices; *don't*; short orders said kindly.
**Gold:** Orla's list from memory; nothing stolen, nobody frightened; off the beach before the helicopter.
**Opens:** the trick **Look!**; the Hum meter; photo homework from the aliens.

## 3.2 NEXT TIME YOU PAY
**From:** Sal's old line *"...but next time you pay"*, the doctor's *"You should get a licence"*, the old game's
driving instructor. **Where:** the Ferry Landing taxi bay; the stadium's Tailgate Lot; the city centre. **Giver:**
S. **Length:** 25 min.

**You do:** learn to drive properly from a man who talks too fast, pass a test given entirely by voice, take your
first fare.

**Beats**
1. **Sal**, on the phone, one breath: *"Youdrovetothehospital? Nolicence? Buddy!"* **YOU:** *"Sal. Slowly,
   please."* **Sal:** *"You. Drove. No. Licence. Come to the ferry. Now."*
2. **The Tailgate Lot.** Empty on a weekday, lines on the ground, Sal's cones. His taxi has a second brake on the
   passenger side and he uses it a lot. He shouts like the camp guard, only kinder:
   *"Forward! Slow. Slower! Stop. Back. Left, left, left. Good! Now park between the cones."*
   When he gets excited the words run together again, and you say it again: *"Slowly, please."* It becomes the
   rhythm of the lesson. (If your CV said you can drive, this part is five cones long and Sal says *"OK, OK, you
   are good, I see it."*)
3. **The test.** An examiner with a clipboard, in the back seat of Sal's taxi. No map, no arrow, no pins. Only
   what she says, once each, in a clear flat voice:
   *"Turn left at the next junction."* *"Take the second street on the right."* *"Stop behind the white van."*
   *"Park in front of the bakery."* *"Go straight on at the lights."*
   Around the city centre: the towers, the courthouse, the construction site. A wrong turn is a mark, not a fail.
   Six marks is a fail, and Sal buys you a coffee and you go again tomorrow.
   **Examiner:** *"Stop here, please. ... You passed."* A licence, with your photo from the camp on it.
4. **The first fare.** Sal gets in the back. *"Now you drive. I watch. Ferry Landing."* A sailor with a bag gets
   in at the taxi bay. You say Sal's line: **YOU:** *"Where to?"* `(old gold line)` He tells you (a real place
   on the map, said once). You drive him there. He talks about home. **YOU:** *"Here we are."* `(old gold line)`
   He pays.
5. **The debt.** You hold the money out to Sal: the fare from day ten.
   **YOU:** *"The gate. Day ten. It was on Uncle Sam."* **Sal:** *"Ha! You remember!"*
   **YOU:** *"This one is on me."* `(old gold lines, both)` Sal takes one dollar and gives the rest back.

**Carries:** *turn left at, the second on the right, behind, in front of, between, straight on*; ordinal numbers.
**Gold:** no marks on the test; the fare delivered without asking him to repeat.
**Opens:** Coronado Cab (OPEN_WORLD.md); the trick **Come here!** (it calls your car or bike); no more risk when
the police see you driving.

## 3.3 FIFTY WORDS
**From:** Ivan, camp day 9. **Where:** Big Al's Tires, the car pound and the vacant lot (gritty edge). **Giver:**
I. **Length:** 25 min.

**You do:** get a friend's car out of the pound, either the legal way or at night, with a partner who only
understands the fifty words you both learned behind a fence.

**Beats**
1. A message from a number you do not know: a photo of two orange camp shoes and one word. **FRIEND?**
2. **Big Al's Tires**, the night shift. Ivan fills the doorway. *"OK, OK! We are friends!"* He bought a car with
   his first pay: old, loud, his. He parked it under a sign he could not read. It is in the pound. The paper says
   **$300**. He has forty. He shows you the paper as if you were a lawyer. You are the one who can read it.
3. **Two ways.** The game means both.
   - **Pay.** Go to the pound office in the morning with $300 of your own. Read the form for him, fill it in
     (name, address, the number plate, the date), sign. Slow, clean, dear. Ivan owes you and does not forget.
   - **Night.** Ivan's plan has four words: *"Night. Fence. Car. Go."*
4. **Night.** You are the eyes and the reader, Ivan is the strength. He does exactly what you say, at once, so say
   it well. Every command is a camp word:
   **YOU:** *"Wait."* *"Go."* *"Down!"* *"Up."* *"Over."* *"Under."* *"Quiet!"* *"Left."* *"Behind the van."*
   - One guard with a torch, walking a square. A camera on a pole.
   - Ivan lifts the bottom of the fence with one hand. You go under. **YOU:** *"Open!"* at the gate from inside.
   - **"Look!"** puts the green blink at the far end of the yard, and the torch goes that way.
   - If Ivan is seen, he stands very still and says *"Hello, my friend!"* `(Omar's old line: it is the only
     greeting he knows)`. It works once.
5. **The clamp.** A yellow clamp on the wheel. Ivan looks at you. Goo, in your ear, bigger this week: *"Say:
   UP."* **YOU:** *"Up!"* The whole car lifts a metre off the ground, turning slowly, green underneath. The clamp
   hangs in the air under it and falls off. Ivan crosses himself.
   The Hum meter jumps. A searchlight starts to cross the yard. **YOU:** *"Down!"* between two containers, in
   the dark, until it has gone over.
6. **Out.** No lights, the back streets of the gritty edge, Ivan in the passenger seat because he bought a car
   before he learned to drive one. (Sal will be told.)
7. **The vacant lot.** **Ivan:** *"You. Me. Friends. Yes?"* **YOU:** *"We are friends."* `(old)` He pays the
   only way he has: he shows you how to stand and how to hit, properly this time.

**Carries:** *wait, go, up, down, over, under, behind, quiet*: short orders, timing, clear speech; reading a form.
**Gold:** never seen; the car down before the light crosses it. (Or: paid, with every box on the form right.)
**Opens:** the trick **Up! / Down!**; the vacant lot fights; *Call Ivan*.

## 3.4 MAYBE WE WILL MEET AGAIN
**From:** Grace's goodbye; camp days 4 and 7. **Where:** Christ Church Community Center; then the docks, the
apartment band, the hospital, Tenth Street Park. **Giver:** G. **Length:** 35 min, in pieces.

**You do:** find five people across the island, fix whatever is stopping each of them, and teach a class for five
minutes.

**Beats**
1. The book you left on a chair comes back to the library. A note inside, in a hand you know from a board in a
   yard: *"Thank you. Tuesday at seven. Bring a friend."*
2. **Tuesday.** The hall. Chairs in a circle. Two students: an old man asleep, and you.
   **Grace:** *"Hello! I am Grace. I am your teacher."* `(old)` She says it the same way. Then she laughs.
   **YOU:** *"Hello, Grace."* She looks at your ear. *"What is that? In your ear."* **YOU:** *"A teacher."*
   *"Is it good?"* **YOU:** *"No."* **Goo:** *"BLUB!"*
3. A letter from the city on her table. Fewer than ten students by Friday and the hall goes to a yoga class. She
   has two. **Grace:** *"They are all here. On the island. They are tired. They work. They are afraid."*
   Five names on the board. Each is a short job somewhere new:
   - **Omar**, at the Shift Canteen in the docks. His line does not close until seven. **YOU:** *"I can help."*
     Twenty minutes on the serving line with him: dock workers, fast orders (*"Chicken, rice, no sauce."*), and
     the line gone by half past six.
   - **Lina**, at Adela Wash & Fold. Forty ironed shirts for the Bayview Inn before she can leave. They go on the
     book bike, in a pile higher than your head. Do not drop them.
   - **Mei**, at the Atrium Cafe in the hospital. *"I am very tired."* `(old)` She says yes, gets on the bus and
     falls asleep on your shoulder. The stops are called out loud. Hear yours, and wake her.
   - **Yusuf** is the old man asleep in the hall. He comes every week and understands nothing. He is in Tenth
     Street Park in the day, playing chess against himself with a stone for one pawn. If you still have the
     white bottle top from the camp: **YOU:** *"This is your pawn."* He holds it for a long time.
   - **Ivan** comes if 3.3 is done. He takes two chairs.
4. **Friday.** Eleven people in a circle, most of whom you fed, carried or woke up. The city's clipboard goes
   round. **Grace:** *"Good. Now you teach."* You. Five minutes. *"Tell them how to take the bus."*
   The chairs become a bus. You are the driver. **YOU:** *"Good morning! Where to?"* **YOU:** *"One ticket,
   please."* **YOU:** *"Sit down, please."* **YOU:** *"This is your stop."* They copy you. Ivan gets it wrong
   and everybody laughs, and he laughs.
5. After, stacking chairs. **Grace:** *"The app on your phone. The lessons. That is my voice."* *"I made it. It
   is not a teacher. People are teachers."* She says the old line again and now it sounds like an order:
   *"In this world, you can talk to every person."* `(old)`

**Carries:** inviting (*Can you come? Do you want to come?*), days and times; food orders; hearing a stop name;
teaching what you know.
**Gold:** all five on Friday; Mei woken at the right stop; the shirts clean.
**Opens:** class nights every Tuesday; the camp four's own small stories (OPEN_WORLD.md).

## 3.5 THE RENT
**From:** the housemate's old line *"The rent is in two weeks!"* **Where:** home; Little Frenchy, high street.
**Giver:** H. **Length:** 25 min. **Opens:** day 23.

**You do:** work a Friday night dinner rush as someone else's hands.

**Beats**
1. Day 23. The rent is tomorrow: $400 from each of you, in the landlord's box. **{H}** holds up the bandage.
   *"I can't hold a knife. If I don't work tonight, I lose the job. If I lose the job..."* A look up at the
   ceiling: the reports, the $90, the room. *"Can you cook?"*
   **YOU:** *"Yes, I can."* / *"No, I can't."* **{H}:** *"It doesn't matter. I talk. You do."*
2. **The kitchen at Little Frenchy.** The chef calls every ticket once, loud, fast, and does not like new people:
   *"Two soup, one fish, one steak, no salt on the steak! Go!"*
   {H} stands behind your shoulder and says it again, small and clear, the way you have been asking the whole
   island to talk to you: *"Two soups. One fish. One steak. No salt."* For the first time {H} is working for you.
3. **The rush.** Four stations: the soup pot, the grill, the plates, the pass. First this, then that: *"First the
   fish. Then the sauce. Then the plate."* Tickets stack up.
   **YOU:** *"How many soups?"* **{H}:** *"Three!"* **YOU:** *"How much sauce?"* **{H}:** *"A little!"*
   - A plate slides off the pass: **YOU:** *"Stop!"* It hangs. You take it out of the air. Nobody saw.
   - Seven tickets at once: Goo, bigger again: *"Say: SLOW."* **YOU:** *"Slow!"* Three seconds in which the
     flames lean and the chef's mouth opens very slowly, and you plate four dishes.
   - The chef finds a hair, a cold plate, a salted steak: each one is tip money gone.
4. The last table of the night is one man by the window, alone, in a clean white uniform. He sends his thanks to
   the kitchen. On the bill, in very small fast handwriting: *"After day thirty. Coffee? Overlook Cafe. D.R."*
5. **The count.** Tips on the steel counter at midnight. With a good night it is enough. With a bad one you are
   short, and there are ways: Sal will lend it, {F} will lend it, Payday Loans 4U will lend it for a price you
   cannot read. If you told {H} to stop the reports, the pile has to be $90 higher.
6. Two envelopes in the landlord's box. **{H}:** *"You paid. I talked."* **YOU:** *"We paid."*

**Carries:** food, numbers and amounts, *how many / how much*, *first ... then*, *no salt*; listening at speed
with help.
**Gold:** no plate sent back; the rent made on tips alone.
**Opens:** the trick **Slow! / Fast!**; kitchen shifts at Little Frenchy; rent every ten days.

## 3.6 THE HAT
**From:** the aliens *"come back every three days"*; Zib's mistake in 1.6 (the stadium is a hat). **Where:** the
stadium: the fan plazas, the Tailgate Lot, Stadium Walk. **Giver:** the green dot. **Length:** 20 min.

**You do:** take two aliens to a game night, hear what the men following you are saying, and get everybody out by
the gate nobody is watching.

**Beats**
1. **Ship night.** **Orla:** *"I want to come too. Zib says there is a big hat. With people in it."*
   **YOU:** *"It is not a hat. It is a stadium."* **Orla:** *"Zib wrote: hat."* Zib holds up the notebook.
2. **Game night.** Ten thousand people in green and white, faces painted, foam hands. Nobody looks twice at a
   very tall woman in a long coat who says hello to everyone:
   **Orla:** *"Hello. I am from forty light years away."* *"Ha! Me too, sister!"*
   Orla wants to practise for her own day forty. She tries the things she has heard in your Reports:
   **Orla:** *"I want a job."* (to a hot dog seller) *"Can I have... one of everything, please?"* *"How was your
   day? Tell me everything."* You fix what you can. **YOU:** *"She wants one hot dog."*
3. **Two men.** No green, no paint, wires in their ears, on the far side of the plaza. Goo, the size of a melon
   now in the sink at home, a drop of it humming in your ear: *"Say: LISTEN. Quietly."*
   **YOU:** *"Listen."* The crowd noise drops away and two voices come close, as if they stood beside you:
   *"...the tall one in the coat. White van. Gate C. Nine fifteen."*
   A place, a time, a thing. If you caught all three you know where not to be and when. If not, say it again and
   get one more line, and lose twenty seconds.
4. **Out.** It is ten past nine. Gate C is the near one.
   - **YOU:** *"Look!"* at the big scoreboard: a green flash, and ten thousand heads turn.
   - **YOU:** *"Orla. Zib. Come here. This way."* Through the crowd the wrong way, along Stadium Walk, to the bus
     stop on the far side. Zib knows what to do with a bus.
   - If the van finds you: on foot through the Tailgate Lot between parked cars, **"Stop!"**, **"Up!"** on a
     barrier, and a helicopter light sweeping the lot.
5. **The bus.** Three seats at the back. The island going past in the dark.
   **Orla:** *"What is day forty? It is on your paper. It is on our clock."*
   **YOU:** *"I have a test. They ask me questions. If it is good, I can stay."*
   **Orla:** *"We want the test too."* **YOU:** *"You can't. You have no papers."*
   **Orla:** *"You had no papers."*
   Nobody says anything. Zib writes two words and shows you: *no papers.*

**Carries:** hearing a place, a time and a thing in a crowd; *want to*; saying something for someone; *can't*.
**Gold:** all three caught the first time; out before 9:15; no green seen by the men.
**Opens:** the trick **Listen.**

## 3.7 TAKE IT EASY
**From:** the officer's tests; Rhodes's card. **Where:** City Hall Front Desk; Overlook Cafe, Glorietta Ring Park.
**Giver:** O, then nobody you would choose. **Length:** 15 min.

**You do:** pass the second check-in, then sit across a table from the man who will not slow down, and answer
him.

**Beats**
1. **Day 30.** The Release Desk. The same three questions, and you are better at them. **Reyes** ticks faster.
   *"Rent?"* **YOU:** *"Paid."* *"Job?"* **YOU:** *"I work at the library. And I drive a taxi."* *"Trouble?"*
   The stamp. **DAY 30.**
2. He does not give the paper back yet. *"Day forty is not here. It is at the courthouse. A judge. Not me."*
   *"Somebody asked for that."* He does not say who. *"You have to tell the judge who you are. In English. You.
   Nobody can do it for you."* A card: **Island Legal Aid**, next to the courthouse. *"You have to stay out of
   trouble. Ten days."*
3. **The Overlook Cafe.** A terrace over the bay. The bridge on one side, the marina below. He has ordered for
   you already. *"On Uncle Sam."* He knows the line.
4. He puts photographs on the table one at a time, like cards. A bus, stopped, green at the edges. A car a metre
   off the ground in a pound. A small figure in a hat on a beach. A tall woman in a coat at a stadium.
   And he talks. Goo is bigger now, so this time you get most of it:
   **Rhodes:** *"Now don't get me wrong, I'm not out to rain on anybody's parade. But you're in over your head,
   and I think you know it. I'm the only one at this table who can pull strings. So let's cut to the chase."*
   **Goo:** *"He says... rain. A parade. Your head is under water. He has strings. I have most of it. Blub."*
5. Then he does something he has never done. He slows down. One sentence, each word alone, so clear that Goo says
   nothing at all:
   **Rhodes:** *"Give me the ship, and you stay. Today. No day forty."*
   He puts a small green card on the table. It has your photo on it.
   *"You have to choose."*
6. **The choice.**
   - **YOU:** *"No."* He picks the card up. Fast again: *"Then I'll see you in court. Mind how you go: two
     strikes is a slippery slope."* **YOU:** *"Slowly, please."* He stands, smiles, and does not.
   - **YOU:** *"I have to think."* The same, and he leaves the photographs. *"Sleep on it."*
   - **YOU:** *"Yes."* You take the card. It is real plastic. On the back, in print too small to read without
     Goo: *TEMPORARY. VOID WITHOUT HEARING.* Goo reads it to you. Then Goo says *"Blub,"* very quietly, and the
     drop in your ear goes still. No voice under the words. No tricks. You told him where the green comes from,
     and by the time you are home there is a van outside. (Chapter 4 begins the same either way. If you said
     yes, you begin it alone, and 4.2 is how you put it right.)
7. He leaves you with the bill after all.

**Carries:** *have to*; saying no; hearing fast speech and getting most of it; small print.
**Gold:** three ticks from Reyes; *"No."*

**End of chapter 3.** Ten days left. The Hum meter never quite empties now.
