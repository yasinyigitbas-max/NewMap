Version 2 (GTA rework). Read `MISSIONS_1_PROLOGUE_CH1.md` first for how a mission is written. **CUT** = a
cutscene (clips in `CUTSCENES.md`). **PLAY** = you have the controls. `(old)` = the old game's line, word for
word.

This chapter is the old game's chapters 4 to 8 in the old order, with the first check-in and the first shoot-out.

Lines about the housemate are written in the *she / her* form (Emma). The *he / his* form (Jack) is the same line
with the pronoun changed, as in the old game's `_f` / `_m` pairs.

## 2.7 KEEP AN EYE OUT
**From:** new. **Where:** City Hall to the courthouse block's parking garage; the commercial strip; Bayside
Express Wash. **Giver:** nobody. It starts as you leave 2.6. **Length:** 18 min.

**You do:** find out you are being followed, get boxed in, and use the Puddler for the first time.

- **PLAY: the tail.** You walk out of City Hall. A white van with no name on it pulls out when you do. Turn; it
  turns. Take a car; it keeps two cars back. Indoors it waits. It is very polite.
- **CUT 2.7-1 "Just a Word"** (30 s). Wherever you go, it ends the same: the ramp of the courthouse parking
  garage, a second van across the exit. Four men in white get out. They have things that are not guns but are
  held like guns. The nearest one, pleasant, quick: *"Evening, sir, the Commander would appreciate a quick word,
  no trouble at all, if you'd just like to step this way and..."* **Goo:** *"I have: 'word'. I have: 'step'."*
  A pause. *"Student. The present."*
- **PLAY: the garage.** Three levels of concrete, pillars and parked cars. The first shoot-out, and it teaches
  itself:
  - **Cover** behind a pillar, a car, a ticket machine.
  - **The Puddler.** A man in white becomes a green puddle with a hat on it. Twenty seconds. Say it as you fire
    (**"Puddle!"**) and the two beside him go too.
  - **Their nets.** A net gun puts you on the floor; shake out of it.
  - **The Stopper** on the van coming up the ramp at you.
  - Eight of them in the end. Take their van, or any car on level two, through the barrier and out.
- **PLAY: four stars.** The Navy's first proper chase: two vans and, for the first time, a helicopter's light on
  the road ahead of you. The game says one thing: **CAR WASH.** Down Adela to the strip, into **Bayside Express
  Wash**, through the brushes and out the other side wet, shining and nobody.
- **CUT 2.7-2 "No Hard Feelings"** (35 s). The garage, later. Eight puddles draw themselves back up into eight
  men in white, sitting, blinking. One is wearing another's shoes. One has a ticket machine receipt stuck to his
  head. On a sprinkler pipe above them, a pigeon. Cut to your kitchen: the phone, face up on the table,
  ringing. **Rhodes**, fast, warm: *"No hard feelings, water under the bridge, boys will be boys. But you and I
  both know that wasn't a water pistol. Be seeing you."* **Goo**, from the sink: *"He is not angry. That is
  bad."*

**Carries:** nothing new. It is a fight. (*"Puddle!"* and *"Stop!"*, shouted, about forty times.)
**Gold:** never netted; out of the garage in three minutes; clean at the car wash on the first pass.

**End of chapter 2.** This is where the old game stopped. Everything after this is built from what it left open.
