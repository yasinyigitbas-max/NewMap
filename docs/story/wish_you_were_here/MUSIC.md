# MUSIC: *Wish You Were Here*

## The idea of the score
- **One melody carries the whole game: the Postcard Theme** (`MUS-01`).
  - It's a short, sunny, slightly sad tune that a person could whistle, because Ari whistles it: it's the
    siblings' childhood song.
  - It's whistled in the prologue, half-finished at the last postcard, completed on the Bridge, whistled by Ari at
    the reunion, sung by a hundred riders at the pier, and whistled again at the end.
  - If a player hums one thing after the game, it should be this.
- **The island's sound:** sunny West Coast (ukulele, surf guitar, handclaps, cumbia from the taco window, a
  Vietnamese cassette in Minh's garage, gospel in Mrs. Okoro's kitchen). The island is many people's music, all at
  once.
- **The villain's sound: Zippy's jingle.** A bright five-note corporate chime ("ding-ding-da-ding-ding") that plays
  whenever the app speaks.
  - In Act 3 the jingle is slowed down and reversed to make the Night Orders theme.
  - In Act 4 it is played by the riders' choir: the villain's sound taken back.
- **All the lyrics are in A1 English, on purpose.** The players are beginners; the songs should be the first songs in
  English they understand all the way through. Short lines, simple words, lots of repetition. They are the best
  vocabulary teachers in the game, and they get stuck in your head.

**Rules for the composer:**
- In dialogue, keep the 1–4 kHz speech band clear. At A1 every word counts.
- Exploration cues loop in 8 or 16 bars, with variations.
- Cues marked *stems* must be delivered in layers, so the game can add instruments as the story adds people.

## Cue list

### MUS-01 "Wish You Were Here (Postcard Theme)"
- **Used:** P1 (the full first statement), M1 (two notes), M6 (minor version), the M7 photo-match sting, M17 (Ari
  whistles it), M21 (the choir), the epilogue.
- **Mood:** sunny, a little homesick, hopeful.
- **Tempo / key:** 92 BPM; G major (the minor version in E minor).
- **Instruments:** solo nylon-string guitar (fingerpicked) and a **whistler** (a real person, not a synth; slightly
  imperfect, breathy). On the Bridge (end of P1): strings, a soft kick and a bass join.
- **The melody's shape:** 8 bars. It rises like a question in bars 1–4 and answers in bars 5–8.
- **The P1 trick:** at the last postcard, the whistle stops on bar 4: the question without an answer. On the Bridge
  it plays bars 5–8 at last.
- **Variations:**
  - M6 end: minor, faster, with a pulse (the mystery begins).
  - M7 "Photo Match": a five-second sting of bars 1–2.
  - M17: whistled *a cappella* by Ari's actor in a small, dead room. It must sound private.

### MUS-02 "Island Morning" (daytime exploration: the hill, the high street, the strip)
- **Mood:** sun, bikes, neighbours, breakfast.
- **Tempo / key:** 104 BPM; C major.
- **Instruments:** ukulele, handclaps, a warm bass, a Wurlitzer piano, glockenspiel, a light shaker.
- **Loop:** 16 bars × 3 variations.
- **Night version (`MUS-02n`):** the same chords on a Rhodes, 84 BPM, brushes, a far-away surf guitar.

### MUS-03 "Ari's Room"
- **Mood:** absence; love in objects.
- **Tempo / key:** 60 BPM; D major.
- **Instruments:** felt piano, a soft music-box doubling the top notes, room tone.
- Plays only in Ari's room, all game. After M17 a second version adds the whistle: Ari is back.

### MUS-04 "The Hub" (diegetic: the hub's speakers)
- **Mood:** corporate fun, *too* happy.
- **Tempo / key:** 120 BPM; F major.
- **Instruments:** bright synth pop, claps, a whistle hook, the Zippy jingle in the chorus.
- **Lyrics (the company song, playing on a loop; original, A1):**
  > *Zipp, Zipp, we go for you,*
  > *Rain or sun, we go for you,*
  > *Big or small, we bring it all,*
  > *Zipp, Zipp, we go for you!*
- (It's meant to become annoying. In M22 the riders sing it at the party with new words: "Lemon, Lemon, we go for
  *us*!")

### MUS-05 "The Bench" (diegetic: Tía Lupe's radio)
- **Mood:** night, neon, friends, cheap tacos.
- **Tempo / key:** 96 BPM; cumbia; A minor.
- **Instruments:** an accordion, a güira, a bass, a crackly AM-radio filter.
- **Loop:** 32 bars. It plays every time the player sits on the bench, as a "home" signal.

### MUS-06 "Sofia's" (the bakery)
- **Mood:** warm bread, early morning.
- **Tempo / key:** 3/4 waltz; 138 BPM; F major.
- **Instruments:** accordion, mandolin, pizzicato bass.

### MUS-07 "Photo Match" (sting)
- The `MUS-01` bars 1–2 on celesta with a soft chime. 5 seconds. It plays whenever a postcard photo lines up with
  the real place.

### MUS-08 "Suds" (the laundromat, night)
- **Mood:** lonely, late, a little dangerous.
- **Tempo / key:** 78 BPM; lo-fi; B-flat minor.
- **Instruments:** a dusty drum loop, a jazzy electric piano. **The washing machines are the percussion** (record
  real machines; build the beat on their thump).

### MUS-09 "Pier at Sunset"
- **Mood:** lazy, golden, salt air.
- **Tempo / key:** 76 BPM; swing; E-flat major.
- **Instruments:** slide guitar, an upright bass, brushes, waves and gulls mixed in.

### MUS-10 "Five Stars" (the riding anthem: Rush orders, M11, and the base of M15 and M19)
- **Mood:** speed, freedom, a little punk.
- **Tempo / key:** 168 BPM; surf-punk; E major.
- **Instruments:** two surf-punk guitars, a fast bass, a driving kit, handclaps, a gang chorus.
- **Lyrics (original, A1; the gang shouts the hook):**
  > *Verse*
  > *Left on Ocean, right on Main,*
  > *Up the hill and down again,*
  > *Bag on my back and the sun in my eyes,*
  > *One more door and one more ride!*
  >
  > *Hook (gang)*
  > *GO! GO! GO! Five stars!*
  > *GO! GO! GO! Five stars!*
  > *Here you are, have a nice day,*
  > *GO! GO! GO! I'm on my way!*
  >
  > *Verse 2*
  > *Pizza, flowers, one banana,*
  > *Big hotel and a dog called Biscuit,*
  > *I don't know the words, but I know the streets,*
  > *One more door, I'm on my feet!*
  >
  > *Hook ×2*

### MUS-11 "Boathouse"
- **Mood:** dark water, dust, a secret.
- **Tempo / key:** free; A minor.
- **Instruments:** celesta playing `MUS-01` very slowly, water drips, low strings held.

### MUS-12 "Night Orders" (the Act 3 theme)
- **Mood:** the friendly voice turned cold.
- **Tempo / key:** 90 BPM; D minor.
- **Instruments:** a synth bass, a slow heavy kick, **the Zippy jingle slowed to a quarter speed and reversed** as the
  main motif, a high glassy pad, distant traffic.

### MUS-13 "Minh's Garage" (diegetic: a cassette)
- **Mood:** joy, family, memory.
- **Tempo / key:** 116 BPM; a 1970s Vietnamese pop style; G major.
- **Instruments:** a surf-style electric guitar, a Farfisa organ, a girl-group vocal (sung in Vietnamese: commission
  an original song in that style; it's Minh's favourite).
- (Composer: this is an original song in a period style, not a cover.)

### MUS-14 "Sixty Seconds"
- **Mood:** a held breath.
- **Tempo / key:** 120 BPM; atonal around C.
- **Instruments:** a ticking hi-hat, a rising string cluster, a heartbeat kick. Exactly 60 seconds, synced to the
  timer.

### MUS-15 "Heat" (the chase)
- **Mood:** danger at speed.
- **Tempo / key:** 172 BPM; E minor.
- **Instruments:** `MUS-10`'s riff, distorted; a breakbeat; siren samples pitched to the key; no gang chorus (the hero
  is alone).
- **Stems:** the friends who help in the chase each bring a layer back (Tomás's whistle, Minh's megaphone sample,
  the party's horns), so the music fills up as the island helps.

### MUS-16 "Dawn" (the end of Act 3)
- **Mood:** a decision made at sunrise.
- **Tempo / key:** 70 → 100 BPM; G major.
- **Instruments:** solo piano playing `MUS-01`, then a drum kit fades in and builds, then stops on a held chord under
  the title card.

### MUS-17 "The Plan" (*stems*)
- **Mood:** a team coming together; fun; momentum.
- **Tempo / key:** 108 BPM; funk; A minor.
- **Instruments:** bass and drums, then one instrument for each friend given a job:
  - Minh: a horn stab;
  - Tomás: a cavaquinho;
  - Noor: a synth arpeggio;
  - Bea: a concertina;
  - Mrs. Okoro: a gospel organ.

### MUS-18 "Dig" (diegetic: Mrs. Okoro's hymn and Bobby's drum)
- **Mood:** funny, holy, midnight.
- **Tempo / key:** 84 BPM; B-flat major.
- **Voice:** Mrs. Okoro's actor sings, unaccompanied at first; Bobby joins on a single drum.
- **Lyrics (an original hymn, A1):**
  > *Dig, dig, dig in the night,*
  > *The Lord is with us, the moon is bright,*
  > *What is lost, we find, we find,*
  > *What is buried comes to the light.*
- The player digs on the beat.

### MUS-19 "Twelve Doors" (the finale run; *stems*)
- **Mood:** everything at once; joy and danger.
- **Tempo / key:** 168 BPM; E major (`MUS-10`'s key).
- **Instruments:** `MUS-10` at full power, plus brass, plus strings, plus **a choir chanting the GO! GO! GO! hook**.
  Each passport delivered adds voices (12 stems: the choir grows from 3 voices to about 60). Fireworks-style
  orchestral hits on the deliveries.
- At Ray's fake Zippy message (Phase 4): everything cuts to the Night Orders motif, then Ari's phone call, then
  everything comes back at once.

### MUS-20 "Small Words" (the pier)
- **Mood:** many people, one quiet truth.
- **Tempo / key:** 72 BPM; G major.
- **Instruments:** **voices only**. A choir of real non-professional singers with many accents singing `MUS-01` on
  "oh". No drums, no instruments. Start with one voice and grow to many as the riders step forward.

### MUS-21 "Sunday" (the party; diegetic, the garden band)
- **Mood:** a loose, happy Sunday.
- **Tempo / key:** 100 BPM; C major; a loose party band (Bobby on drums, a guitar, Tomás singing, everyone clapping).
- **Lyrics: "Small Words"** (original, A1; Tomás sings, the garden joins the chorus):
  > *Verse 1*
  > *I came with a bag and a postcard,*
  > *I didn't know one word,*
  > *Now I know "hello" and "thank you",*
  > *And "sorry", and "where", and "here".*
  >
  > *Chorus*
  > *Small words, big heart,*
  > *Every friend is a place to start,*
  > *Say it slow, say it true,*
  > *Small words, I love you.*
  >
  > *Verse 2*
  > *A grandma on a bike, a cat on a boat,*
  > *A dog with a paw on the door,*
  > *A taco at ten and a song at the bench,*
  > *I don't need a lot, I don't need more.*
  >
  > *Chorus ×2 (the last time everyone sings, and the Zippy jingle plays on a kazoo)*

### MUS-22 "Wish You Were Here" (end credits)
- **Mood:** the whole story: distance, love, a new home, a letter home.
- **Tempo / key:** 92 BPM (the Postcard Theme's tempo); G major.
- **Instruments:** it opens with the whistle and the nylon guitar exactly as in P1. Then a voice (a warm, slightly
  accented singer: a person who learned English, audibly). Then the full band (bass, drums, a Wurlitzer, strings),
  then a choir in the last chorus.
- **Lyrics (original, A1):**
  > *Verse 1*
  > *Here is a photo, here is the sea,*
  > *Here is a house with a lemon tree,*
  > *Here is a street, and here is a door,*
  > *I am not lost any more.*
  >
  > *Chorus*
  > *Wish you were here, wish you were here,*
  > *The sky is blue and the night is clear,*
  > *I have a bike, I have a friend,*
  > *Wish you were here, wish you were here.*
  >
  > *Verse 2*
  > *I don't have many words to say,*
  > *But I say them every day,*
  > *Hello, thank you, here you are,*
  > *Small words go very far.*
  >
  > *Chorus*
  >
  > *Bridge (the music drops to the guitar and the whistle)*
  > *One day you take the bus,*
  > *One day you cross the bridge,*
  > *And I will be at the station,*
  > *And I will say your name.*
  >
  > *Final chorus (the choir)*
  > *Wish you were here, wish you were here,*
  > *The sky is blue and the night is clear,*
  > *I have a home, I have a friend,*
  > *Wish you were here… and you will be here.*
  >
  > *Outro: the whistle alone, bars 1–8 of the Postcard Theme, complete.*
- **Note:** the bridge says "I will", the only future tense in the game, on purpose. It's a little A2 gift at the
  very end, a promise of the sequel.

## Radio (on scooters, at the Bench, in shops)
Each station has DJ links in slow, clear "radio English" for beginners and 6–8 original songs with A1 choruses.
| Station | Genre | DJ | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coronado 101** | Sunny pop, surf, indie | Two morning hosts, slow and clear on purpose ("Good morning, Coronado! It is sunny! It is Monday!") | The weather and traffic reports name real streets on our map: real A1 listening |
| **Radio Lupe** | Cumbia, norteño, bolero | Tía Lupe's nephew, Chuy | The Bench's station |
| **Night Zipp** | Lo-fi and electronic, for riders at night | Zippy reads the time between songs ("It is two o'clock. You are doing great.") | In Act 3 it gets creepy; in M22 it becomes **Lemon Radio**, run by Noor |
| **Home Radio** | Music from the player's own region (the localisation) | none | Comfort; the only station with no English. Always there, never pushed |
