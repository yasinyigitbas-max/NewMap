## Props that carry the story
- **The postcards:**
  - 24 postcards, each a photo or drawing on the front and handwriting on the back;
  - one blank postcard for the epilogue;
  - a postcard font in Ari's handwriting, and one in the player's.
- **Passports:** dark red booklets with orange sticker numbers (generic and invented, never a real country's
  design).
- **The biscuit tin** (the buried box).
- **The orange Zipp jacket** (the hero's: Ari's) and an older faded one (Ari's hoodie).
- **Ari's notebook.** **The cookbook** *Simple Food for Busy People* with the pencil numbers.
- **A hub key card.** **The orange keyring.**
- **Mr. Pemberton's banana**, **Biscuit's paw print**, **Bea's bowline knot**.

## Sounds to record
- A real **whistler** for the Postcard Theme (and Ari's actor whistling it in M17).
- **Washing machines** (the laundromat beat).
- **Zippy's jingle** and Zippy's voice (synthetic but warm; a text-to-speech style, clearly a machine).
- The 911 operator call (calm, slow, trained).
- A **gospel hymn** sung by Mrs. Okoro's actor, unaccompanied.
- **Many accents saying one sentence each:** record real people from many countries for M21's rider lines and the
  M21 choir.
- Scooter and e-bike motors, a bike bell, rigging on masts, a dinghy's oars, a roll-up garage door.
