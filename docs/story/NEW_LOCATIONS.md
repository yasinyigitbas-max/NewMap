# New locations, props and sounds the story needs (Version A)
Yasin offered to build anything. This is the list, ordered by when the story first needs it.

For each entry:
- **Where:** the district and the closest kit zone.
- **Needs:** props, interiors, sounds.
- **Why:** the story reason.

"Kit" means it can probably be dressed from the existing city kit. "New" means it needs new modelling.
