# A1 map: *The Long Way Round* (A1 edition)
Yasin's scenario order, matched to the A1 syllabus. The rules for every player line are the A1 contract in
`docs/story/wish_you_were_here/A1_MAP.md`:
- structures taught so far only;
- at most 8 words a sentence in Acts 1–2 and 10 in Acts 3–4;
- the A1 word list plus names, the job words (order, fish, fryer, tip, shift, rent) and up to 5 new words a mission,
  each shown first;
- fixed chunks allowed: *Sorry? / Slower, please. / Here you are. / Keep the change. / It's on me. / Can I use the
  toilet, please? / I don't understand. / No problem. / Let's go.*;
- joiners: *and* from Act 1, *but* from Act 2, *because* from Act 3;
- no future tenses;
- grammar never fails a line: the NPC answers the meaning and recasts the right form once.

**New** is the structure a mission is built round (met many times, said at least three times). **Practice test**
is Yasin's five-question test (scrambled sentences plus voice) that Pocket offers when the mission ends: optional,
never a wall.
