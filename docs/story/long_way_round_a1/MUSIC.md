# MUSIC: *The Long Way Round* (A1 edition)
The score is the first draft's score: `docs/story/MUSIC.md` has every cue's tempo, key, instruments, loops and stems,
and the cue ids are the same. This file lists what this edition uses and what changed:
1. **The songs are rewritten in A1 English.** The players are beginners, so every sung word should be one they can
   understand by the end of the game: short lines, simple words, lots of repetition.
2. **Some cues are gone** with the B1–B2 scenes, and a few cues have new places (below).

## The idea (unchanged)
- **Two melodies.**
  - **The Sea** (`MUS-01`): a bowed fiddle (kamancheh, or erhu or violin without vibrato), the hero's home and the
    journey.
  - **The City** (`MUS-04`): the same melody in a major key on a bright trumpet over a sunny West-Coast groove.
  - The story is one melody's journey from the sea to the city. They meet in `MUS-27` (the chase) and `MUS-34` (the
    credits).
- **Marek's theme** (`MUS-11`): frame drums and a button accordion, instruments from "home". The villain sounds like
  home.
- **In dialogue,** keep the 1–4 kHz speech band clear. At A1 every word counts.

## The songs, with A1 lyrics (all original)

### MUS-05 "Benny's Radio": the station's hit (Benny sings it badly)
- Sunny Afrobeat guitars, yacht-rock keys, a talking drum, horns, a smooth male lead. 112 BPM, A major.
> *Sun on the street, sun on the sea,*
> *Windows down, it's you and me,*
> *Left and right and round we go,*
> *Coronado, nice and slow.*
>
> *Nice and slow, nice and slow,*
> *Coronado, nice and slow!*
- Benny sings "Corona-DOH" every time. The chorus is the M1 joke (*slower, please*), sung.

### MUS-28 "Home": the dawn after the rescue
- Acoustic guitar, soft piano, a warm folk voice a little rough at the edges, harmonies in the chorus, the fiddle in
  the bridge. 74 BPM, G major.
> *Verse 1*
> *I was a name on a paper,*
> *I was a number in a line,*
> *I was a face in the water,*
> *Now the morning is mine.*
>
> *Chorus*
> *Home is a door, home is a light,*
> *Home is a friend on a long, long night,*
> *Home is a word that I can say,*
> *Home is here, home is today.*
>
> *Verse 2*
> *Somebody said "it's on me",*
> *Somebody gave me a bed,*
> *Somebody threw me a circle,*
> *White in the dark, and I said…*
>
> *Chorus*
>
> *Bridge (the fiddle, then softly)*
> *The sea is big, the sea is old,*
> *But the sea gives back what the sea can hold.*
>
> *Chorus ×2 (the last time with no instruments)*

### MUS-34 "The Long Way Round": end credits (the main theme with words)
- It opens with the fiddle alone (the Sea melody, 8 bars), then the full band (drums, bass, Hammond, tremolo guitar),
  the trumpet melody, strings, and a gospel choir from the second chorus, with Marek's frame drum underneath. The
  singer has an accent from *somewhere*, audibly: a person who learned English. 104 BPM, D major (the fiddle intro in
  D Dorian).
> *Verse 1*
> *One word on a grey boat,*
> *A blanket on my back,*
> *I didn't have my English,*
> *I didn't have a map.*
>
> *Pre-chorus*
> *Slower, please, can you say it again?*
> *Say it again, my friend…*
>
> *Chorus*
> *I took the long way round,*
> *Over the water, into the town,*
> *Every word is a stone on the ground,*
> *I made a road, the long way round.*
>
> *Verse 2*
> *I cook the fish, I drive the car,*
> *I know the street, I know the door,*
> *I know the people with the light on,*
> *I'm not alone any more.*
>
> *Pre-chorus*
>
> *Chorus*
>
> *Bridge (the music drops to the fiddle and the voice)*
> *There is a short way, it's easy, it's fast,*
> *But the short way doesn't last.*
> *There is a long way, it's slow, it's hard,*
> *And it takes you where you are.*
>
> *Final chorus (the choir; the key goes up a step)*
> *I took the long way round,*
> *Over the water, into the town,*
> *Every word is a stone on the ground,*
> *Now my feet are on the ground,*
> *I took the long way round.*
>
> *Outro: the fiddle alone, the Sea melody in a major key. The last note is held, as in P1.*

The DJs speak slowly and clearly on purpose ("Good morning, Coronado! It is sunny! It is Monday!"). Every song has a
simple, repeating chorus.
