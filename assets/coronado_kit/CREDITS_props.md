The hedge body (hedge_body) is baked in Blender from a high-poly scatter of ~30k privet leaves whose colours are sampled from the
shrub_01 photo (foliage/_tools/hedge_bake.py). Far-tier impostor slots are renders of our own near-tier models.
Mapillary images were not used as pixels anywhere (colour / wear reference only).

Masonry regions (15 Nov 2026): `brick` and `cmu` were rebuilt at real scale by props/_tools/masonry_regions.py. Brick faces come from
red_brick and mortar from concrete_floor_02. CMU split faces come from concrete_block_wall (regraded to light grey-tan). Grime is from dirty_concrete.
All sources are Poly Haven, CC0 1.0. No new sources were added.

Phase D lettering (15 Nov 2026): the water tower, billboard, headstone, obelisk, substation sign and skate tag lettering were rendered from Google Fonts
families Cinzel, Anton, Sedgwick Ave, Rubik Spray Paint and Permanent Marker (SIL Open Font License 1.1). They exist only as baked pixels
in the props atlases. No font files ship with the kit. The OFL places no restriction on rendered output. No new photo texture sources were added for phase D:
every surface reuses the CC0 Poly Haven sources listed above.

Tiling trim regions (15 Nov 2026): props/_tools/trim_regions.py builds periodic 128 px/m trim tiles for the big city props.
Page B: tb_paint (painted steel, tinted per prop by vertex colour), tb_galv, tb_concrete, tb_rust, tb_grate. Page A: ta_concrete, ta_stucco.
The sources are painted_concrete_02, rusty_painted_metal, rust_coarse_01, dirty_concrete, concrete_floor_02 and white_stucco, all
Poly Haven, CC0 1.0. The tiles are high-passed, wrap-blended and made periodic from those photos. The bar grating (tb_grate) is
synthetic. No page grew and the compression is unchanged.

Graffiti decals (15 Nov 2026): fence_graffiti_cmu_panel carries two spray-paint decals (graffiti_piece "KRWN" (renamed from "CORO", see below), graffiti_tag "Krus 06";
both names are fictional). props/_tools/graffiti_regions.py draws them into the free page A strip from Rubik Spray Paint and Sedgwick Ave
(SIL Open Font License 1.1, rendered pixels only). Outline, fade, highlights and drips are procedural. No photo or Mapillary pixels are used.

Gate name plate and skate wear (15 Nov 2026): the gate_plate decal region ("BAYVIEW CEMETERY", a fictional name) is rendered from Cinzel
(SIL OFL 1.1, rendered pixels only) by props/_tools/gate_plate_region.py. The faint skate scuffs on ta_concrete (props/_tools/skate_scuffs.py)
are procedural strokes. Trim tiles are tone-matched to their material regions (props/_tools/trim_match.py). No new photo sources were added.
